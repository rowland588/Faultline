# The gemba walk

## Purpose and principle
Leaders go to where the work is to see, ask and learn; what is seen becomes
problems and actions; and how long since anyone looked is itself shown.

## Questions it answers
When did someone last look at this line? What did they find?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The walk's observations (photos, film, notes) and when.

## Procedure and calculation
Days since last walked; observations raised and closed.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Walks as inspections; nothing done with what was seen.

## Reading the result
"Line 3 not walked for 9 days."
