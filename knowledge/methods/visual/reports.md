# Reports and the lean page

## Purpose and principle
Every report and tool page on one pattern: the band (what, where, when), the
tiles (counted figures of what they should be, since the record before), the
trend, the tool's picture, the top causes, what it means, the action plan and
the closing line — on screen and on paper from one model.

## Questions it answers
Where are we, why not where we should be, what are we doing about it — on one page.

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The tools' records.

## Procedure and calculation
Laid out by the report engine (`src/lib/report/`), never a hand-kept y.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Reports that list records instead of telling the story.
