# Daily management

## Purpose and principle
A daily rhythm: an hour-by-hour board at the line (plan, actual, reason), a
short tiered meeting (line, area, site) on safety, quality, delivery, cost and
people, escalation within the day, and leaders' standard work to keep it going.

## Questions it answers
Are we on plan this hour? What went wrong yesterday, who is fixing it?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Hourly plan and actual; the day's issues; the actions.

## Procedure and calculation
Hour gaps with reasons; SQDC statuses; actions raised and closed.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Meetings reporting without acting.

## Reading the result
"11:00 hour 410 of 480 — film change ran over; action on the reel trolley."
