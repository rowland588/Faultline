# The KPI tree (the lever tree)

## Purpose and principle
A number to hit broken into the drivers that make it, down to levers a team
can pull, each with its own measure, so effort goes where the number moves.

## Questions it answers
What has to be true for us to hit the number? Which lever is off track?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The outcome and target; the drivers and their measures.

## Procedure and calculation
The tree; each lever's target and actual.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Drivers that do not multiply or add up to the outcome.

## Reading the result
"Cost a pack 31p → 28p: labour 9p → 8p (on track), give-away 4p → 2p (off track)."
