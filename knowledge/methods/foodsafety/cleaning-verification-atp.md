# Cleaning verification — ATP and swabs

## Purpose and principle
Surfaces swabbed after cleaning and read (ATP in RLU, or a lab count); results
against pass, caution and fail limits, trended by site.

## Questions it answers
Is cleaning effective, which sites fail most?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Swab sites, results, limits.

## Procedure and calculation
Pass rate by site; Pareto of fails; trend.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
ATP limits are device- and site-specific; not a microbial count.

## Reading the result
"Slicer blade fails 3 of 10 — redesign the clean."

## When not to use it — and instead
Pathogen control → `foodsafety.environmental-monitoring`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Slicers, conveyors, depositors.
