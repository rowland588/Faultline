# Traceability exercise

## Purpose and principle
From a raw-material lot forward to every customer, and from a finished lot
back to every raw material, with a mass balance, timed — to prove a recall would work.

## Questions it answers
Can we trace, how completely, how fast?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The lot to trace; records; the start and end times; quantities in and out.

## Procedure and calculation
Time taken; % of the lot accounted for.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Testing the easy lot every time.

## Reading the result
"Traced 99.6% of the lot in 2 h 40 m."

—

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Every food site at least annually.
