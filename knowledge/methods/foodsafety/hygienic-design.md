# Hygienic design review of equipment

## Purpose and principle
New or modified equipment reviewed against hygienic-design principles
(cleanable, drainable, no harbourage) before purchase and at acceptance.

## Questions it answers
Can this machine be cleaned properly? What must the OEM change?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Drawings or the machine; the guideline checklist.

## Procedure and calculation
Findings by severity; closed before handover.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Reviewing after installation, when changes are costly.

## Reading the result
"9 findings; 2 harbourage points under the infeed — OEM to fix before SAT."

—

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
New lines, especially ready-to-eat areas.
