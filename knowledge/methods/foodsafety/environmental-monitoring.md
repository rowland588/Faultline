# Environmental monitoring

## Purpose and principle
Swabs from zones by risk (1: food contact to 4: outside the area) for indicator
organisms and pathogens; positives trigger investigation, and trends show harbourage.

## Questions it answers
Where are positives, are they recurring, are we in control?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Sites with zones, results from the lab, dates.

## Procedure and calculation
Positives by site and zone; recurrence; time to negative.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Sampling only after cleaning; ignoring zone 3–4 positives.

## Reading the result
"Two zone-3 positives at drain 4 in a month — recurring."

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Ready-to-eat sites: sandwiches, salads, cooked meats.
