# CCP monitoring against its critical limit

## Purpose and principle
Each CCP checked at its frequency against its critical limit, a failure
triggering the corrective action and product review — and, beyond pass/fail,
how close to the limit it runs (capability), so a CCP drifting towards its
limit is seen before it fails.

## Questions it answers
Was every check done and passed? How close to the limit does it run?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The check schedule; each reading with who and when; the limit.

## Procedure and calculation
Checks done ÷ due; failures; one-sided capability on the readings.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Probes not verified; readings rounded to the limit.

## Reading the result
"Core temperature: 96 checks, none under 75 °C; Ppk 1.9 against it."
