# Cooling curves — time to temperature

## Purpose and principle
Core temperature read at intervals as product cools; the time to cross each
limit, against the site's cooling standard.

## Questions it answers
Does every batch cool fast enough? Which loads are slow?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Temperature readings with times; the limits.

## Procedure and calculation
Interpolated time to each limit; one-sided capability (at most) on the times.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Probe placement not at the slowest point.

## Reading the result
"Median 84 min to 8 °C, 1 in 10 over 110 — the deep trays."
