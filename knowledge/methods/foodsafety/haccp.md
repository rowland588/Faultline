# HACCP

## Purpose and principle
The seven principles: hazard analysis; determine CCPs; critical limits;
monitoring; corrective actions; verification; documentation — a preventive
system for food safety.

## Questions it answers
Which hazards are controlled where, to what limit, and is it monitored?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The process flow, hazards, the team.

## Procedure and calculation
Decision tree for CCPs; the plan's rows.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
A plan written for the audit, not used on the floor.
