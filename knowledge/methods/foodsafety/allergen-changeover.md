# Allergen changeover validation

## Purpose and principle
After an allergen product, a validated clean, inspected and tested (lateral
flow or lab test) before the next product; the validation proves the clean
works, verification proves each clean was done.

## Questions it answers
Was the clean done and verified? How long does it take, and can SMED shorten it safely?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The clean's steps; inspection; test results; who released.

## Procedure and calculation
Pass/fail per clean; time from end of run to release.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Shortening the clean without re-validating.

## Reading the result
"12 allergen changeovers, all passed; median 74 minutes to release."
