# Metal detection and X-ray verification

## Purpose and principle
Test pieces of each metal type at the agreed sizes passed with product
(leading, middle, trailing) at the agreed frequency; each must be detected and
rejected, with the reject confirmed.

## Questions it answers
Is the detector working at the agreed sensitivity, every check?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The schedule; each check's test piece, position, detected, rejected.

## Procedure and calculation
Checks done ÷ due; any failure → product since the last good check held.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Testing without product; passing a check proves function, not how often metal is missed.

## Reading the result
"All 24 checks passed; 1 missed check at 14:00 — product held."
