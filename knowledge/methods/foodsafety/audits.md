# Scored audits — GMP, hygiene and layered process audits

## Purpose and principle
A fixed checklist walked at a frequency, by different levels (layered), each
finding scored and actioned, the score trended.

## Questions it answers
Is the area at standard, is it improving, are findings closed?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The checklist; each audit's answers, photos and actions.

## Procedure and calculation
Score %; findings open; repeat findings.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Audit for the score, not the finding.

## Reading the result
"GMP 86%; 4 findings open, 1 repeat."

—

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Daily GMP walks, weekly hygiene audits.
