# Cost of poor quality

## Purpose and principle
Quality's costs in four buckets — prevention, appraisal, internal failure
(scrap, rework, rejects), external failure (complaints, credits, recalls) —
so quality is argued in money.

## Questions it answers
What does poor quality cost us a month, and where?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Costs by bucket from the records (rejects × cost, rework hours, credits).

## Procedure and calculation
Totals by bucket and line; as a share of sales or of cost.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Only the visible costs counted; prevention cut to "save".

## Reading the result
"£38 k a month; internal failure £27 k — rejects on line 3 the most."

## When not to use it — and instead
Plant-wide losses of every kind → `equipment.cost-deployment`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Rejects and write-offs, retailer credits, recalls.
