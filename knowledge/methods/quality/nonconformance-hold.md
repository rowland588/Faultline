# Product on hold — non-conformance, disposition and release

## Purpose and principle
Product that may not conform is identified and held, its disposition decided
(release, rework, downgrade, destroy) by someone authorised, and the cost recorded.

## Questions it answers
What is on hold, since when, why, and what has it cost?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Each hold: product, quantity, reason, who, when; its disposition and cost.

## Procedure and calculation
On hold now; days held; cost by disposition.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Holds released without a recorded reason.

## Reading the result
"4 pallets on hold 3 days for weight review; £2,100."

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Positive release holds, metal-detector failure holds, label-error holds.
