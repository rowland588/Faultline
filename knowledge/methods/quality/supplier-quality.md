# Supplier quality — incoming checks and supplier PPM

## Purpose and principle
Materials checked on arrival and each supplier's defects counted per million
received, so supplier problems are seen and argued with numbers.

## Questions it answers
Which supplier's film causes the most line problems?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Deliveries, quantities, incoming checks, rejections and line problems traced to material.

## Procedure and calculation
PPM = defective units ÷ units received × 10⁶; trend by supplier.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Line problems not traced to the material lot.

## Reading the result
"Supplier B's film: 2,100 PPM, 6 line stops traced."
