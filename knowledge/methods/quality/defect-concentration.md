# Where on the pack — the defect concentration diagram

## Purpose and principle
Each defect marked where it was found on a drawing or photo of the product, so
clusters show the cause's location (one jaw corner, one lane).

## Questions it answers
Where on the pack do the leaks start?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
A picture of the product; each defect's position.

## Procedure and calculation
Counts by region; the heaviest region.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Positions guessed afterwards rather than marked when found.

## Reading the result
"Two-thirds of leaks start at the front-left corner — jaw 2's corner."

## When not to use it — and instead
Defect types → `quality.check-sheet`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Tray seal leaks; print defects on film; bruising on produce.
