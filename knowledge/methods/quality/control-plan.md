# Control plan

## Purpose and principle
For each characteristic that matters: the specification, how it is measured,
how often, by whom, the record, and the reaction when it is out — the
sustain step of every improvement.

## Questions it answers
How is this kept under control, and what happens when it is not?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The characteristics (from FMEA, HACCP, customer specs).

## Procedure and calculation
A row per characteristic; checks due and done.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Reaction plans that say "inform supervisor".

## Reading the result
"Fill weight: 5 packs every 30 min, X̄-R; out → hold since last good check, adjust, recheck."

—

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Weights, seals, codes, metal detection, temperatures.
