# Check sheet — defects tallied by type at the line

## Purpose and principle
Each defect found tapped against its type as it is found, with the amount
inspected, so the counts are honest and ready for a Pareto and a rate.

## Questions it answers
Which defects, how many, out of how much?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The defect types (a short fixed list plus "other, said"); the amount inspected.

## Procedure and calculation
Counts by type; rate by type = count ÷ inspected.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Types added on the fly without control; amount inspected not recorded.

## Reading the result
"In 500 packs: 9 leakers, 4 mislabels, 2 crushed."

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
