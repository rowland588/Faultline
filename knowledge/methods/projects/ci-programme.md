# The improvement programme

## Purpose and principle
Ideas from the floor and projects tracked from raised to done, with proven
savings by month, so the programme's worth is seen.

## Questions it answers
How many ideas, how many done, what did they save, proven?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Ideas and projects with status, owner, saving claimed and proven.

## Procedure and calculation
Counts by status; savings proven against claimed.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Claimed savings reported as proven.

## Reading the result
"41 ideas, 18 done; £62 k proven of £95 k claimed."
