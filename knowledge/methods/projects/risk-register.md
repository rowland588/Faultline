# Risk register

## Purpose and principle
Risks to a project with likelihood, impact, owner and response, reviewed
regularly; decisions and changes logged with who agreed them.

## Questions it answers
What could go wrong, how bad, who is watching it?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Risks, scores, owners, responses.

## Procedure and calculation
Score = likelihood × impact; top risks reviewed.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Registers written and never read.
