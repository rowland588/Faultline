# Operational readiness

## Purpose and principle
Before production takes a new line: people trained and signed off, standards
and SOPs written, spares and maintenance plans in place, HACCP and allergen
controls updated, cleaning validated.

## Questions it answers
Is production ready to take this line, and what is still missing?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The readiness list with owners and evidence.

## Procedure and calculation
Items ready ÷ items; open items by owner and due date.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
A list ticked without evidence.

## Reading the result
"23 of 31 ready; HACCP review and cleaning validation outstanding."

## When not to use it — and instead
The machine's own tests → `commissioning.acceptance-tests`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
HACCP review of the new line; validated cleaning; training of operators and hygiene staff.
