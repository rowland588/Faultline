# Layout and material flow — the from–to chart

## Purpose and principle
Moves between areas counted (from × to), times distance, so the layout that
cuts the heaviest flows can be found.

## Questions it answers
Which areas should sit next to each other?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Moves between areas a day; distances.

## Procedure and calculation
Load-distance = Σ moves × distance; compare layouts.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Ignoring hygiene zoning (high and low risk) — in food it comes first.

## Reading the result
"Moving the film store beside line 3 cuts 1.2 km a day."

## When not to use it — and instead
One person's walk → `lean.spaghetti`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Packaging stores, intake, despatch — inside hygiene zones.
