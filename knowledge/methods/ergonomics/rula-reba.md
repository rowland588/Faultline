# RULA and REBA — posture scores

## Purpose and principle
Posture of each body part scored from observation (or a photo), with force and
repetition, into an action level.

## Questions it answers
How bad is this posture, and how soon must it change?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The worst or most common posture; load; frequency.

## Procedure and calculation
Tables A, B, C of each method → a grand score and action level.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
One posture snapshot standing in for a shift.

## Reading the result
"RULA 6 — investigate and change soon."

## When not to use it — and instead
Lifting → `ergonomics.niosh-lifting`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Reaching into deep totes; packing at low benches.
