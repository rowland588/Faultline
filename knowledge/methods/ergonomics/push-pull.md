# Pushing and pulling

## Purpose and principle
The forces to start and keep a trolley or pallet truck moving, against limits
for the population, and the route's factors.

## Questions it answers
Is moving these racks of trays a risk?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Measured forces (a force gauge read by a person); route; frequency.

## Procedure and calculation
Forces against the tables or RAPP's scoring.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Measuring on a clean floor once.

## Reading the result
"Initial force 310 N on the ramp — high."

—

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Rack trolleys in bakeries; pallet trucks.
