# HSE's MAC and ART

## Purpose and principle
UK regulators' screening tools: MAC scores lifting, carrying and team handling
by factor (load and frequency, distance, posture, grip, floor, environment) in
colour bands; ART does the same for repetitive arm and hand work (frequency,
force, posture, breaks, duration).

## Questions it answers
Which handling and repetitive tasks on the line need action first?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Observation of the task against each factor.

## Procedure and calculation
Factor colours and scores summed per the HSE charts.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
A screen, not a full assessment.

## Reading the result
"Case packing ART score 26 — red on frequency and posture."
