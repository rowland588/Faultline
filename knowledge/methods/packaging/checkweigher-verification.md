# Checkweigher verification

## Purpose and principle
At start-up and at intervals, known test packs (in limit, under limit) run
through the checkweigher at speed to show it weighs correctly and rejects
what it should.

## Questions it answers
Is the checkweigher accurate at speed, and does the reject work?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Test packs of known weight; the times; each result.

## Procedure and calculation
Error per pass; reject confirmed or not; results trended.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Testing static, not at line speed.

## Reading the result
"Under-limit pack rejected 3 of 3; error +0.4 g at speed."

## When not to use it — and instead
The gauge's capability → `msa.type1-gauge`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Every checkweigher on an ℮ line.
