# Code and label verification

## Purpose and principle
Date codes, allergen declarations and barcodes checked at start-up, after each
change and at intervals, because a wrong label is a recall.

## Questions it answers
Was every check done, did every check pass, and what happened when one failed?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The check schedule; each check's result, who and when.

## Procedure and calculation
Checks done ÷ due; failures and their containment.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Checks signed without looking; scanners not verified.

## Reading the result
"All 18 hourly checks passed; changeover check failed once — 40 packs reworked."

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
