# Spaghetti diagram — walking and transport distance

## Purpose and principle
A person's (or a product's) path drawn on the layout over a cycle or a
shift; the tangle shows the waste of motion and transport, and the distance
measured makes it a number.

## Questions it answers
How far does the operator walk a shift? Which trips could go?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The layout to scale; the path followed for a cycle or a timed period.

## Procedure and calculation
Distance per cycle × cycles a shift = distance a shift; time = distance ÷ walking pace.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
One person's cycle may not be typical; follow several.

## Reading the result
"2.4 km a shift, 40% fetching film."

## When not to use it — and instead
Many products between many places → `ergonomics.layout-from-to`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Packers fetching film reels and cases; hygiene staff between stations.
