# 5S — sort, set in order, shine, standardise, sustain — audited

## Purpose and principle
Workplace organisation as the base of standard work and visual control:
what is not needed goes, everything has a marked place, cleaning is
inspection, the standard is written, and audits keep it.

## Questions it answers
Is the area at its standard, and is it holding?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
A scored checklist per area, the same questions each time; who audited, when.

## Procedure and calculation
Score per S (e.g. 0–4 per question), as a share of the maximum; trend by audit.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Scores drift with the auditor; layered audits and photos keep them honest.

## Reading the result
"Line 3 at 72%, down from 81%; shine the weakest."

## When not to use it — and instead
Food-safety audits with their own clauses → `foodsafety.audits`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Shadow boards for colour-coded cleaning tools; designated places for knives and blades.
