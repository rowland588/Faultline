# Improvement kata and coaching kata

## Purpose and principle
A daily routine: understand the direction, grasp the current condition, set
the next target condition, experiment one step at a time (each a small PDCA
with its prediction), coached by the five questions.

## Questions it answers
What is the target condition, where are we, what is the next experiment and what do we expect?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The challenge, the current condition, the target condition with its date, an experiment log.

## Procedure and calculation
Each experiment: step, expected, what happened, what was learned; the measure trended.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
It is a practice routine; filling the form without coaching misses it.

## Reading the result
"Experiment 7: cut the film splice to one person — expected 30 s, got 45 s; learned the reel is too heavy."

## When not to use it — and instead
A one-off big change → `lean.kaizen-event`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
A team leader's daily improvement on line output or give-away.
