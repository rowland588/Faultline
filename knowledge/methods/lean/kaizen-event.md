# Kaizen event — a focused improvement week with its follow-up

## Purpose and principle
A team released for a few days on one scoped problem: baseline, find waste,
change, measure again, standardise — with a 30-day check that it held.

## Questions it answers
Did the week's changes move the number, and is it holding a month on?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
A charter (scope, target, team), the baseline measure, the after measure, the open actions.

## Procedure and calculation
Before → after on the same measure; actions closed at 30 days.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Events without follow-up decay; a series of events is not a system.

## Reading the result
"Changeover 42 → 24 min; 3 of 11 actions still open at 30 days."

## When not to use it — and instead
Ongoing daily improvement → `lean.toyota-kata`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
A week on a filler's changeover; a week on packing-hall flow.

## References
- [textbook] Imai, M. (1986), *Kaizen: The Key to Japan's Competitive Success*, McGraw-Hill.
