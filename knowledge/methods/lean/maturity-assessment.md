# Lean and operational-excellence maturity assessment

## Purpose and principle
A structured assessment of how far an operation's practices and behaviours
have come, against a model's levels, to choose what to develop next.

## Questions it answers
Where are we strong and weak as a lean operation?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
A chosen model, its criteria and the evidence for each.

## Procedure and calculation
Score per dimension against the model's levels.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Models differ and are often proprietary; scores are judgements.

## Reading the result
"Daily management level 2 of 5; problem solving level 3."

## When not to use it — and instead
For a line's numbers, use the tools themselves.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Group-wide manufacturing excellence programmes.
