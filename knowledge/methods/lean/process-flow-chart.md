# Process flow chart — operation, transport, inspection, delay, storage

## Purpose and principle
Every step of one product or person, classed by five symbols — operation,
transport, inspection, delay, storage — with distance and time, so the
non-operations (the waste) stand out and can be questioned one by one.

## Questions it answers
How many of the steps change the product? How far does it travel? Where does it wait?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The steps as followed on the floor, each with its class, time and distance.

## Procedure and calculation
Totals per class (count, time, distance); share of time in operations.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
One path only; parallel work needs a multiple-activity chart.

## Reading the result
"23 steps, 6 operations; 140 m travelled; 71% of the time waiting."

## When not to use it — and instead
The whole stream → `lean.value-stream-mapping`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
A tray of product from oven to packing; an ingredient from intake to weigh-up.
