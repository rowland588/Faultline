# Is / is not — specifying the problem

## Purpose and principle
What, where, when and how much the problem *is*, against what it could be but
*is not*; the distinctions between the two, and the changes behind them, point
at the cause.

## Questions it answers
On which lane, shift, product does it happen — and not happen? What changed?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Facts about occurrences and non-occurrences.

## Procedure and calculation
A table: is / is not / distinction / change; test each possible cause against it.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Filled with opinion instead of facts.

## Reading the result
"Leakers on lane 3, not lanes 1–2; since Tuesday; new jaw fitted Monday on lane 3."

## When not to use it — and instead
Many causes to explore → `rca.fishbone-5whys`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Sudden spikes in rejects or complaints.
