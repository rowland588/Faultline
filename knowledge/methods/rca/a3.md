# A3 — the problem and its solution on one page

## Purpose and principle
Background, current condition, target, root-cause analysis, countermeasures,
plan and follow-up, told on one sheet so the story is clear and agreed.

## Questions it answers
What is the problem, why, what are we doing, and did it work — at a glance?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The analysis and the plan.
