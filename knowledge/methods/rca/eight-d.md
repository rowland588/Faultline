# 8D — eight disciplines of problem solving

## Purpose and principle
A structured report for a significant problem, often for a customer: team,
problem description, containment, root cause, corrective actions chosen,
implemented and verified, prevention of recurrence, team recognised.

## Questions it answers
What did we contain, what is the root cause, how do we know it will not recur?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The problem's facts; the containment; the root-cause analysis.

## Procedure and calculation
D0–D8 sections, with dates.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Written to close a complaint rather than solve the problem.

## Reading the result
"D3 contained 14 pallets; D4 root cause: worn jaw on lane 3; D6 verified 0 leakers in 6 weeks."

## When not to use it — and instead
Internal improvement → the 6M.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Retailer complaints, foreign-body incidents.
