# PDCA — plan, do, check, act

## Purpose and principle
Each change as an experiment: plan it with its expected effect, do it, check
the effect on the number, and act — standardise or try again.

## Questions it answers
Did the change do what we expected? Is it held?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The expectation written before; the measure before and after.

## Procedure and calculation
Before → after (`stats.two-sample-tests`); holding over a period.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Skipping "check": changes declared successes without measuring.

## Reading the result
"Expected −30% breaks; got −61%; holding 4 weeks — standardised."
