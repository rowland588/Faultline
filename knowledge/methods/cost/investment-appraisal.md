# What an improvement is worth — payback, NPV and the case for new equipment

## Purpose and principle
A change's one-off cost against what it saves or earns each period: payback
period, net present value at a discount rate, internal rate of return.

## Questions it answers
Is this fix worth doing? When does it pay back?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
One-off cost; saving a period (from the cost model); life; discount rate.

## Procedure and calculation
Payback = cost ÷ saving a period; NPV = Σ savingₜ/(1 + r)ᵗ − cost.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Savings claimed off the constraint as output gained.

## Reading the result
"£4,800 for the splice table saves £310 a week: pays back in 16 weeks."
