# The skills matrix

## Purpose and principle
People against tasks or machines with their level (learning, can do with
help, can do alone, can teach), so cover gaps show before an absence finds them.

## Questions it answers
Who can run the wrapper on nights? Where is one absence a stopped line?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
People, tasks, levels, dates trained.

## Procedure and calculation
Per task: people at level 3+ per shift; gaps below the target (often 3).

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Levels never refreshed.

## Reading the result
"Only one person on nights can set up the labeller."
