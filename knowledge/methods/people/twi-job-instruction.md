# TWI job instruction

## Purpose and principle
A job broken into important steps, each with its key points (what makes or
breaks it) and the reason for each, taught in four steps — prepare, present,
try out, follow up.

## Questions it answers
How do we teach this job so everyone does it the same, right?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The standard work; the job's experts.

## Procedure and calculation
The breakdown sheet; trainees signed off.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Long SOPs nobody reads instead of breakdowns.
