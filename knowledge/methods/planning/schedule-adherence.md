# Plan attainment — made against planned, by product

## Purpose and principle
What was made against what was planned, product by product and day by day —
the right product, the right amount, at the right time — with the reasons for misses.

## Questions it answers
Did we make the plan? Which products missed, why?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Plan and actual by product and day; reasons.

## Procedure and calculation
Attainment = Σ min(actual, plan) ÷ Σ plan (over-production does not make up for misses).

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Counting over-production of one product against misses of another.

## Reading the result
"91% attainment; the 1 kg missed by 2,400 — the changeover overran."
