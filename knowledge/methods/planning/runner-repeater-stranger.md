# Runners, repeaters and strangers

## Purpose and principle
Products classed by volume and regularity: runners (high, steady — make every
day, fixed slots), repeaters (regular, smaller — a wheel), strangers (rare —
make to order), so each is planned the right way.

## Questions it answers
Which products get fixed slots and which are made to order?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Demand history by product.

## Procedure and calculation
Volume share and coefficient of variation by product; thresholds agreed.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Thresholds arbitrary; review as demand changes.

## Reading the result
"6 runners (78% of volume) get daily slots; 14 repeaters on a 2-week wheel."
