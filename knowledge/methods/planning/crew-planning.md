# Crewing a shift — heads needed, absence and cover

## Purpose and principle
Heads a shift from the work at the planned rate (Line capacity's people
needed), then the employed heads needed allowing for absence, holiday and training.

## Questions it answers
How many people per shift, and how many on the books to cover it?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
People needed by line and shift; absence and holiday rates; skills.

## Procedure and calculation
Employed = needed ÷ (1 − absence − holiday − training shares).

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Ignoring skills — a head is not a trained operator.

## Reading the result
"18 needed a shift; 22 employed per shift at 18% cover."
