# First-off release at changeover — line clearance and the first good pack

## Purpose and principle
After a changeover, the line is cleared of the previous product's packaging
and labels, and the first packs checked — code, label, weight, allergen,
seal — and signed before running.

## Questions it answers
Was the line cleared? Did the first packs pass, who signed, how long did release take?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The release checklist; each check's result and who; the time from changeover end to release.

## Procedure and calculation
Pass/fail per check; time to release; failures by check.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Signed without checking; release time hidden outside the changeover's time.

## Reading the result
"Released 06:42, 11 minutes after the changeover; label check failed once, corrected."

## When not to use it — and instead
Ongoing hourly checks → `packaging.code-label-verification`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Retail label and allergen checks at every product change.
