# Energy and water per tonne

## Purpose and principle
Meter readings taken at intervals by a person, divided by output, so energy
and water intensity are trended and their cost known.

## Questions it answers
Are we using more energy or water per tonne? What does it cost?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Readings with times; output over the same periods; tariffs.

## Procedure and calculation
Intensity = use ÷ output; baseline against production (regression of use on output).

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Ignoring the fixed base load.

## Reading the result
"412 kWh a tonne, up 6%; the base load at night 180 kW."
