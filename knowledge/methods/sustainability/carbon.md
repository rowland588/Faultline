# Carbon a pack

## Purpose and principle
Energy use (scope 1: fuels; scope 2: electricity) converted with the year's
emission factors and divided by output.

## Questions it answers
What is our carbon per pack, and is it falling?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Energy by source; the year's factors; output.

## Procedure and calculation
CO₂e = Σ use × factor; ÷ packs.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Old factors; scope 3 ignored silently.
