# Compressed air leak survey

## Purpose and principle
Leaks found by ear or a hand-held ultrasonic detector (read by a person),
tagged, sized and costed, and fixed.

## Questions it answers
What do our air leaks cost, and are the tags fixed?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Each leak's place and size estimate; the air cost.

## Procedure and calculation
Flow by size × hours × £ per unit of air.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Leaks found and never fixed.

## Reading the result
"48 leaks, about £11 k a year; 30 fixed."

—

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Packaging machines' pneumatics.
