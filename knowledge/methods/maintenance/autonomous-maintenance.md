# Autonomous maintenance — clean, inspect, lubricate, tighten

## Purpose and principle
The people who run the machine keep its basic condition: cleaning as
inspection, lubricating, tightening, and tagging every abnormality found; the
checks are standards with a frequency and a result.

## Questions it answers
Were the checks done? What abnormalities were found and are they fixed?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The check list per machine with frequency and method; each check's result; tags raised and closed.

## Procedure and calculation
Checks done ÷ due; tags open, by age; repeat abnormalities.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Tick-box checks without looking.

## Reading the result
"96% of checks done; 14 tags open, 3 over a week."

## When not to use it — and instead
Engineers' scheduled work → `maintenance.planned-maintenance`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Daily checks on seal jaws, knife condition, conveyor belt tracking — joined to hygiene cleaning.
