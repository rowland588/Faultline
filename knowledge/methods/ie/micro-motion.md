# Micro-motion study — therbligs and the two-handed chart

## Purpose and principle
Very short, highly repeated work studied motion by motion for each hand —
search, grasp, transport, hold, release … — against the principles of motion
economy (both hands working, short reaches, fixed places).

## Questions it answers
Why is this 3-second element slow; what is each hand doing?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Footage of the work, slowed down.

## Procedure and calculation
Each hand's motions charted with times; idle and holding motions questioned.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Effort far beyond its value on low-volume work.

## Reading the result
"The left hand holds for 1.2 s of 3.1 s — a fixture would free it."

## When not to use it — and instead
Elements longer than a few seconds → `ie.time-study`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
High-volume hand placing — topping, garnishing, tray loading.

## References
- [textbook] Barnes, R.M. (1980), *Motion and Time Study: Design and Measurement of Work*, 7th ed., Wiley.
