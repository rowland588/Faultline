# Labour productivity, earned hours and efficiency against standard

## Purpose and principle
Output per labour hour, and the standard hours the output earned against the
hours paid, split into utilisation (on the work) and performance (pace when on it).

## Questions it answers
How many packs a labour hour by line and shift? Are we working to standard?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Output; hours worked (clocked); standard time a unit.

## Procedure and calculation
Productivity = output ÷ labour hours. Earned hours = output × standard time.
Efficiency = earned hours ÷ hours worked; utilisation = hours on standard work ÷ hours paid.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Comparing lines with different automation; standards out of date.

## Reading the result
"212 packs a labour hour on days, 188 on nights; efficiency 87%."

## When not to use it — and instead
The cost of a pack → `cost.line-cost-model`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Labour per tonne in a meat plant; packs per person-hour on packing lines.
