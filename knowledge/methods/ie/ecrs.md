# ECRS — eliminate, combine, rearrange, simplify

## Purpose and principle
Each element questioned in order: can it be eliminated? combined with another?
rearranged? simplified? — the method-study discipline behind most work improvement.

## Questions it answers
Which elements can go, merge or move, and what does each save?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The elements with their times (`ie.time-study`).

## Procedure and calculation
Each element given an ECRS decision and its expected saving; the balance re-worked.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Savings claimed without re-timing.

## Reading the result
"Eliminate the second label check (−2.1 s), combine fold and place (−1.4 s)."

## When not to use it — and instead
Changeovers → `changeover.smed` (its own stages).

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Hand-pack stations, case packing.
