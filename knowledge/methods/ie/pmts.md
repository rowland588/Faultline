# Predetermined motion time systems — MTM and MOST

## Purpose and principle
A job analysed into basic motions (reach, grasp, move, position …), each with
a time from published tables, summed to a standard time — for jobs not yet
done, or to design work before the line exists.

## Questions it answers
How long will this job take before we can time it?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The method described motion by motion; trained, certified analysts.

## Procedure and calculation
Σ table times (in TMU, 1 TMU = 0.036 s for MTM) for the motion sequence.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Licensed data and certification; misuse by untrained analysts is common.

## Reading the result
"Assembly 312 TMU = 11.2 s before allowances."

## When not to use it — and instead
The job exists → `ie.time-study`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Designing a new hand-packing station before the line is built.
