# Standard data — reusing timed elements across products

## Purpose and principle
Elements timed once (place a tray, apply a label) kept with their standard
times and the factors that change them, so a new product's work is built
from them without timing everything again.

## Questions it answers
What will this new pack format's work take, from what we have timed already?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
A library of elements with their standard times and the conditions they hold for.

## Procedure and calculation
Σ library elements for the new job, adjusted by the factors recorded.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Elements reused outside the conditions they were timed in.

## Reading the result
"New 6-pack built from 7 timed elements: 14.6 s."

## When not to use it — and instead
A new method with no timed elements → time it.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Pack-format variants on a hand-packing line.
