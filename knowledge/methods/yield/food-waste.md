# Food waste measured by stage

## Purpose and principle
Food that leaves the factory as waste (not product, not by-product sold)
weighed by stage and cause, so it can be reduced and reported.

## Questions it answers
How much food is wasted, where, why, and what does it cost?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Waste weights by stage and cause; destination (animal feed, AD, landfill).

## Procedure and calculation
kg by stage and cause; % of input; £ at material cost; trend.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Water in waste weighed as food.

## Reading the result
"2.8% of input wasted; overproduction of short-life product the biggest cause."

—

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Bakery overproduction, trim, end-of-run waste, date-expired stock.
