# Yield and mass balance by stage

## Purpose and principle
What goes in by weight against what comes out as product, by-product, rework
and waste at each stage, so losses are found where they happen and the balance closes.

## Questions it answers
Where is the raw material lost? What is our yield against standard?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Weights in and out at each stage over the same period; moisture or cooking loss where relevant.

## Procedure and calculation
Yield = out ÷ in by stage; overall = Π stage yields; unexplained = in − Σ outs.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Weights from different periods; water gain/loss ignored.

## Reading the result
"Peel and trim 18%, cooking loss 9%, packing 1%: 74% overall against 77% standard."

## When not to use it — and instead
Units passed first time → `quality.fpy-rty`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Potato peeling, meat trimming, cooking and chilling loss.
