# Material usage variance

## Purpose and principle
Material used against what the output should have used at standard, in
quantity and money, for ingredients and packaging.

## Questions it answers
Are we using more film or more ingredient than standard, and where?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Issues or usage by material; output; standard usage a unit; cost.

## Procedure and calculation
Variance = (actual − standard × output) × cost.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Stock counts wrong; standards out of date.

## Reading the result
"Film 6.2% over standard — splice waste on line 3."
