# Multihead (combination) weigher performance

## Purpose and principle
The weigher chooses a combination of heads whose sum is closest to the target
(at or above it); its give-away depends on head weights, the number of heads
and product flow. Head-by-head and combination data show which heads or feeds
make the give-away.

## Questions it answers
Why is give-away high on this weigher? Are some heads under-fed? Is the target right?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Pack weights in order; head weights if the weigher shows them; feed settings.

## Procedure and calculation
Give-away, spread and the shape of the pack weights; head means compared (`stats.anova`).

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Normal-tail estimates on a truncated distribution.

## Reading the result
"Give-away 3.8 g; heads 5 and 11 run at half weight — the dispersion cone feeds them poorly."
