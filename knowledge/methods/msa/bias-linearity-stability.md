# Bias, linearity and stability of a gauge

## Purpose and principle
Bias: the average difference from a reference. Linearity: whether bias changes
across the range. Stability: whether it drifts over time (a control chart of a reference).

## Questions it answers
Does the scale read high at the top of its range? Does it drift over the week?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
References across the range; repeated readings; a reference checked over time.

## Procedure and calculation
Bias = x̄ − ref with a t-test; linearity: regression of bias on ref; stability: XmR chart of the reference.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
References themselves out of calibration.

## Reading the result
"Bias +0.3 g at 1 kg, rising 0.1 g per kg."

## When not to use it — and instead
Repeatability across people → `msa.gauge-rr`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Platform scales at intake; dispensing scales.
