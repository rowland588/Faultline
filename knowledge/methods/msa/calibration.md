# Calibration register and checks

## Purpose and principle
Every instrument that judges product has a known accuracy, a due date for
calibration and routine checks between (a probe in ice water, a test weight).

## Questions it answers
Which instruments are due or overdue? Did the last check pass?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The register (instrument, range, tolerance, interval); each calibration and check with result.

## Procedure and calculation
Due and overdue; check results against tolerance; the actions on a failed check (product since the last good check).

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
A failed check with no review of product measured since.

## Reading the result
"3 probes overdue; the blast-chiller probe read +1.4 °C at ice point — out."

## When not to use it — and instead
The gauge's own capability → `msa.type1-gauge`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Cooking and chilling probes (CCPs), checkweighers, intake scales.
