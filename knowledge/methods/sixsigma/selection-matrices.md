# Choosing between solutions

## Purpose and principle
Options scored against agreed criteria (Pugh: better, same, worse than a
baseline; weighted scores), or placed on impact against effort, so the choice is explicit.

## Questions it answers
Which countermeasure first? Which design?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Options; criteria with weights; scores agreed by the team.

## Procedure and calculation
Σ weight × score; or quadrants of impact × effort.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Weights chosen to pick a favourite.

## Reading the result
"Quick wins: a film guide and a reel trolley."

## When not to use it — and instead
Money decides → `cost.investment-appraisal`.
