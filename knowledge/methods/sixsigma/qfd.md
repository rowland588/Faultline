# QFD — the house of quality

## Purpose and principle
Customer requirements related to technical characteristics in a matrix, with
importance, relationships and targets, so design effort follows what the customer values.

## Questions it answers
Which technical characteristics matter most to the customer's needs?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Weighted customer needs; technical characteristics; relationship strengths.

## Procedure and calculation
Importance of each characteristic = Σ need weight × relationship.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Large, slow; better for new products than running lines.

## Reading the result
"Seal strength and peel force carry 41% of the importance."

## When not to use it — and instead
Running-line problems → the 6M.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
New pack design.
