# SIPOC

## Purpose and principle
A one-page scope: who supplies what into which few process steps, producing
which outputs for which customers — agreed before mapping in detail.

## Questions it answers
What is in and out of the project's scope?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
The team's knowledge of the process.

## Procedure and calculation
A table of five columns; 4–7 process steps.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Too detailed — it is a scope, not a map.

## Reading the result
"Intake → wash → slice → pack → despatch; customer: the retailer's depot."

## When not to use it — and instead
The detailed stream → `lean.value-stream-mapping`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Scoping a yield project from intake to packing.
