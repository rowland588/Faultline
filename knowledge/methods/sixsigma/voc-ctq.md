# Voice of the customer to CTQs — and Kano

## Purpose and principle
What the customer says turned into measurable requirements with limits (CTQs);
Kano sorts requirements into must-be, performance and delighters.

## Questions it answers
What exactly must we measure, and to what limits, to satisfy the customer?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Customer specifications, complaints, interviews.

## Procedure and calculation
Need → driver → CTQ with a limit; Kano by paired questions.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
CTQs without limits.

## Reading the result
"'Easy to open' → peel force 8–15 N."

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Retailer specifications; peelable lids; reclose.
