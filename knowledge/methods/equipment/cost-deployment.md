# Cost deployment — losses mapped to causes and money (WCM)

## Purpose and principle
Every loss in a plant listed, separated into causal and resultant losses,
traced to its process, converted into cost, and ranked so improvement
projects are chosen by money and their savings tracked.

## Questions it answers
Where is the plant's money lost, by cause, and which projects buy most?

## Inputs and minimum conditions
Losses measured across the plant (time, material, energy, labour) and the cost rates.

## Procedure and calculation
Loss matrix (losses × processes), causal/resultant split, cost per loss, Pareto, project selection and savings.

## Assumptions, limits and misuse
Heavy to set up; double-counting losses that are results of others.

## Reading the result
"£1.9 m a year in losses; breakdowns on the fillers £410 k — first."

## When not to use it — and instead
One line's shift → `equipment.time-model-losses`.

## In food and FMCG manufacturing
Group-wide WCM programmes in large food plants.
