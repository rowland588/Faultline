# FC-111 — Designed experiments: two-level factorials (a question of the Analysis tool)

**Catalogue:** FC-111 Designed experiments — two-level factorials (a mode of FC-105 Analysis). **Knowledge:** `stats.doe-factorial` (with `stats.anova`, `msa.gauge-rr`). **Programme:** `docs/METHODS.md` §11 (DMAIC Analyse and Improve). **Asked for:** Rowland, 1 November 2026 — *"I want all tools made that get used in the projects."* **Status:** built 1 November 2026 (branch `m6-analysis`).

## 1. Purpose
Settings are changed one at a time and the interaction is missed: the seal is only weak when the dwell is short *and* the temperature is low. A planned pattern changes several settings at once; every combination is run, in a random order, and each factor's effect is read from all the runs together. It is the one way to *prove* a cause on a line, because the settings are moved on purpose. The sentence it lets one side say to the other: *"Temperature adds 3.85 N to the seal and dwell 1.85 N, more at the long dwell; pressure does not matter in these ranges, so it can sit at its cheaper low setting — 16.1 N expected, and a confirmation run to check it."*

**Scope.** One question in the Analysis tool (`kind: 'doe'`), asked first: **Which settings drive the result — and do they interact?** Two to five factors, each at a **low and a high** setting typed by hand (name, unit, low, high); every corner of the full 2^k design run **1 to 4 times** and **0 to 6 centre runs**; the run order **shuffled from a seed that is stored and said**, so a drifting line cannot pass itself off as a factor; the response typed as each run is made (a pad box that says what to set next) or pasted in run order. The page works out the run sheet, the effect of every term (main effects and every interaction), which are real, the settings the fitted model puts best, and — with centre runs — a test for curvature. **With replicates** the noise is the pure error of the runs themselves (the analysis of variance, a t-test on each effect); **with one run a corner** there is no error estimate, so Lenth's pseudo standard error judges the effects against each other. Not in this build: **fractional factorials** (2^(k−p), their generators and aliasing) and Plackett–Burman screening; **blocking**; a model beyond a straight line between two levels (the response surface, FC-112, is the next step the curvature check points to); effects valued in pounds (the response's worth per unit is the response's own — the experiment's own cost is given); several responses at once; a gauge R&R before the first run (FC-118 is its own question — the page only reminds).

## 2. Users and scenarios
The process engineer at a desk planning the trial, then the technician at the sealer with a phone, setting the line to run 7's settings and typing the seal strength. (1) A tray sealer: seal temperature × dwell × pressure on seal strength, one run each (8 runs). (2) A plasma-etch style trial with two replicates (16 runs) where the noise is measured. (3) A filler: speed × product temperature on give-away, with centre runs to see a bend. (4) A paper run sheet for the floor before anything is made.

## 3. Method selection
`stats.doe-factorial`: the design matrix is the columns of −1/+1 in Yates' standard order (the first factor changes fastest), an interaction's column the product of its factors'. The effect of a term is the mean response where its column is +1 less the mean where it is −1: contrast ÷ (r·2^(k−1)); its sum of squares contrast² ÷ (r·2^k); its coefficient in the coded model half the effect. Alternatives and when not (from the record): a relationship in running data, nothing varied on purpose → What moves with what; one factor at several levels → Do groups differ; the best point inside the box → the response surface (FC-112); many factors to screen → a fraction (not built). The page says in words that an effect is for the ranges tried and that a straight line between two levels is all a two-level design draws.

**Established · assumed · chosen.**
*Established:* the 2^k design, effects, sums of squares and the coded model (Box, Hunter & Hunter, *Statistics for Experimenters*, 2nd ed., ch. 5–6; Montgomery, *Design and Analysis of Experiments*, ch. 6); the standard error of an effect with replicates, SE = √(4·MSE ÷ (r·2^k)) on 2^k(r − 1) degrees of freedom (Montgomery ch. 6); Lenth's method (Lenth 1989, *Technometrics* 31(4): s₀ = 1.5·median|effect|, PSE = 1.5·median of the |effects| under 2.5·s₀, margin of error ME = t(1 − α/2, m/3)·PSE, simultaneous margin SME with γ = (1 + (1 − α)^(1/m))/2); the curvature test from centre points (Montgomery §6.8: SS = n_F·n_C·(ȳ_F − ȳ_C)² ÷ (n_F + n_C) against the centre points' own variance); the hierarchy principle (a model keeps the lower-order terms an interaction stands on). The independent cases are NumPy/SciPy's (`scripts/knowledge/cases-m6-analysis.py`) and Montgomery's printed examples (the 2⁴ filtration rate and the 2³ plasma etch — the textbook's effects 21.625, 9.875, 14.625, −18.125, 16.625 and sums of squares 41,310.5625, 374,850.0625, 94,402.5625 reproduced exactly).
*Assumed:* the response is measured well enough (the page names gauge R&R as the check before); the runs really are made in the order given and everything else is held; the readings at a corner are independent; low and high are wide enough to show an effect and safe to run.
*Chosen by Faultline (not the textbooks'):* a term is **real** when its two-sided t-test p is under 1 − confidence (with replicates) or when it is **beyond Lenth's ME** (one run a corner; "firm" beyond the SME) — the confidence is the question's 90 · 95 · 99%, not multiplicity-adjusted across the terms (the table gives every p, the picture every interval); the run order is shuffled by mulberry32 and Fisher–Yates from a seed (a small generator, not a certificate of randomness; the seed is shown); the fitted model is the real terms and what they stand on; the **best corner** is the corner the fitted mean puts best when a direction was given, a factor the model does not use being "either"; its interval is t·√(MSE·p ÷ N) (p parameters, N runs) and given only with replicates; two to five factors, 1–4 replicates, 0–6 centre runs; the design must be **balanced** — every corner the same number of readings — before the effects are worked out (a struck reading leaves it short, and which corner is short is said); an experiment's cost = runs × packs a run × materials a pack, both optional. None of these has been put to Rowland one by one.
*Money (`knowledge/MONEY.md` FC-111):* the experiment's own cost (runs × materials, optional inputs, missing ones named); the **effect in pounds is not given**: it depends on the response (give-away per pack, a reject's cost) — the capability and stratification tools price those; this is said in the limits, not hidden.

## 4. Inputs
*Agreed before measuring:* the response (a name and unit); which way is better (for the best setting only — never changes a test); the factors, two to five, each a name, a unit (optional), a low and a high (the high above the low); times each corner is run (1–4); centre runs (0–6); confidence 90 · 95 · 99%; packs a run makes and materials a pack (optional). The seed is made once and kept. *Measured:* one response per run, in the order the page gives them: Enter in the box, or the whole column pasted in run order; a run struck is kept and left out.

## 5. Data suitability
Balanced corners (the same number of readings at each); the corners are the only runs the effects use, the centre runs only judge curvature (at least two, said). One run a corner works only when most effects are nothing — said. Readings that do not belong to the design (another number of factors, a setting that is neither low, high nor centre) are left out and counted. Identical readings at every corner leave no error: every effect that moves at all is called real, and the page says so.

## 7. Outputs
The lean page: the band (the verdict — *Temperature and Dwell matter*, *Nothing shown to matter*, *Runs to make*, *Not started*), five tiles (Runs done · Real effects · Biggest · Noise s or PSE · Explained), the sentence (the real effects, largest first, each with its interval and p or its margin; the factors not shown to matter, "leave it where it is cheapest"), the lines under it (how the noise was found; the interaction read as two numbers; the settings the model puts best with their interval and "run it to confirm"; the share explained; the experiment's cost), the picture (a lane for each of the eight largest effects on a zero line with its interval or margin), the tables (*Every effect*; *The runs, in the order to make them / they were made*), the checks (no error estimate, curvature, replicates agreed against found, readings that do not belong), the readings, Agreed, the action plan, the closing line. Tone: neutral; amber only when the centre runs show a bend. **Never claimed:** that an effect holds outside the ranges tried; that two levels show the shape between them; that the model's best corner is proved — "run a confirmation".

**The report.** The paper (`src/lib/studiesPdf.ts analysisBlocks`): before any run it is the **run sheet** — the factors with their low and high, the runs in the order to make them with a blank Response column, and the seed; after the runs it is the report — the band and tiles, the sentence and what follows, the picture, *Every effect*, *The runs … in the order they were made* with their responses, the checks, what was agreed in words (*Gap 0.8–1.2 cm …; every corner run 2 times, 3 centre runs, in a random order shuffled from …*), and every reading worded with its run and settings. It reads the same module as the screen.

## 8. Manufacturing usability
Phone first: the next run is said in full ("Run 4 of 8: set Temperature 190 °C, Dwell 1.2 s, Pressure 3 bar") with one box and Enter; the rest of the column can be pasted; the three runs after it are listed. The factor boxes and the questions are a thumb's size. The run sheet prints for the floor before anything is made.

## 9. Failure handling
No name, fewer than two or more than five factors, a repeated name, a missing or non-number setting, a high not above its low, replicates or centre runs out of range: refused in words while agreeing. Unbalanced corners: "N of M runs done … Short: …" naming the corners. A struck reading leaves the experiment short, not wrong. Identical readings at a corner: no spread, said. One centre run: no curvature test, said. A client reads and changes nothing (`can.edit`, `can.agree` as the Analysis tool's).

## 10. Integration
Record: `ToolStudy` of tool `analysis`, `agreed.analysis` (`kind: 'doe'`, `doe: { factors, replicates, centre?, seed, packsPerRun?, materialsPerPack? }`); readings in `facts.readings` as `{ arm: run number, group: time this corner is run, xs: coded levels, value }` — inside the existing JSON, no migration. Shows wherever the Analysis shows (list row *which settings drive it · n runs*, a line's and a project's Tools, DMAIC Analyse and Improve and the directory — FC-111 opens the Analysis marked as a mode). The curvature check points to FC-112. No new page, route or menu line.

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. The question is asked first under its own heading; factors refused in words (none, a high below the low, a repeated name); the run count said as it is planned — `doe.mjs` step 1 and `analysisDoe.test.ts` ("agreeing").
2. The design: standard order, the corner index, the shuffled order from a stored seed equal to a second implementation, every corner and replicate present — `factorial.test.ts` and `doe.mjs` step 2.
3. Montgomery's 2⁴ (one run a corner): effects, sums of squares, Lenth's PSE, ME and SME at 90 · 95 · 99% as NumPy/SciPy print them; the real and firm effects — `factorial.test.ts`.
4. Montgomery's 2³ plasma etch (two runs a corner): SSE, MSE, SE, every effect's SS, t, p and interval; the fitted model's predictions, R², best corner and its interval; the interaction as two numbers — `factorial.test.ts`, `analysisDoe.test.ts` and `doe.mjs` step 5.
5. The bagger (one run a corner): Temperature, Dwell and their interaction real by ±0.565 N, Pressure not; 16.10 N expected with no interval; the page's sentence, tiles and closing line equal the module's — `doe.mjs` step 3.
6. A half-made experiment says how many runs are done and which corner is short and works nothing out; a struck run does the same and a restored one makes it whole — `doe.mjs` steps 3–4 and `analysisDoe.test.ts`.
7. Centre runs: curvature judged against their own spread and said either way — `factorial.test.ts` and `doe.mjs` step 6.
8. The experiment's cost named missing, then in pounds (16 × 200 × £0.12 = £384) — `doe.mjs` step 5.
9. The paper downloaded and read back: blank as the run sheet, full as the report with the effects, the runs, what was agreed and the seed — `doe.mjs` steps 2 and 7.
10. Kept after a reload; the list's row; re-agreeing keeps the order; the phone has nothing off the side and its controls are a thumb's size — `doe.mjs` steps 5 and 8.
