# FC-233 — Micro-movements: the specification

**Catalogue:** FC-233 (a calculator of the shared shape — the two-handed chart and the therbligs; a mode of FC-021's family, standing alone) · **Knowledge:** `knowledge/methods/ie/micro-motion.md`, `docs/LINE-TALK-TOOLSETS.md` §4.4, `knowledge/MONEY.md` §4 (FC-233, as FC-028) · **Asked for:** Rowland, 6 November: *"watching an FLT driver move a box of products from one area to another and doing like the micro movements, or an individual doing micro movements, what they have to do."* · **Status:** built on the repo's branch (see the catalogue entry); no AI anywhere in it.

## 1. Purpose
A very short, very repeated piece of work — a forklift driver moving one box from one area to another, a packer's two hands filling a tray — is slow and nobody can see why. This lists the work as the motions it is made of, in order, each named by Gilbreth's therblig, each with its seconds **only if timed or said**, and says how much of the cycle is working motion, how much is motion to question (search, select, plan, hold, avoidable delay) and how much is checking or allowed time; for two hands, what each hand does and when one hand is only a clamp while the other works. The sentence it lets the lead say to the engineer: *"the left hand holds 2.2 s of a 6.2 s cycle while the right works — give the hold to a fixture."*

**Scope.** (1) **One person or one truck** — a label (*Packer 1*, *Truck 3*; never a name) — and up to **24 elements in the order they happen**, each: *what* (the floor's words), a **class** from the list of fourteen in §3, a **hand** (left, right, both, or whole body / truck; left blank it is *not said*), **seconds only if timed or said**, and, for a left or right hand, whether it **happens together with the element above** (the other hand). (2) The figures in §6: the elements by class and group, the cycle's timed total, the shares in working motion, motion to question and the rest, the longest element, and for two hands the seconds each hand holds, holds while the other works, and has nothing listed. (3) Optionally cycles a shift, what an hour costs and shifts a week — the labour on the cycle's seconds that are only motion to question. (4) The two-handed chart down the page, on the screen and on the paper from one description. **Not in scope:** any predetermined-time table (MTM-1, MOST, MODAPTS — licensed; Faultline never supplies a time, and estimates none, FC-020 stays *left out*); reading the motions from footage (the video study's frames are a later slice, FC-021 mode); a cycle over 24 elements; posture and force (the ergonomic tools FC-207 and the RULA/REBA entries judge them); the route walked (spaghetti, FC-004/FC-242) and the path of the product (FC-003); the improvement itself (the five questions of FC-028 are the person's).

## 2. Users and scenarios
An engineer or team leader at the bench or on the dock with a phone, a stopwatch or the video study's times, or **Line Talk**, which fills the same boxes from a spoken description (T4, a later slice). (1) *An FLT driver moves one box from goods-in to the chiller:* drive empty to the pallet, search for the right pallet, check the label, line up the forks, lift, drive loaded, wait for the door, line up at the rack, set down and back out, wait for the scanner — ten elements, whole body. (2) *A bench packer's two hands put a pouch in a tray:* the right reaches, grasps and moves the pouch while the left reaches for, grasps and **holds** the tray; the chart shows the left hand a clamp. (3) *Said, not timed:* "he reaches for the box, grips it, lifts, turns, walks two steps, sets it down" — the elements counted and classed with no seconds, none made up; the page asks for the stopwatch.

## 3. Method selection
Motion study: the Gilbreths named the elemental motions (therbligs) and drew them for each hand on the simultaneous-motion (simo) chart; Barnes's principles of motion economy say what a good pattern looks like (both hands working at once, no hand used as a clamp where a fixture could do it, a search or a select designed out). The alternatives (`knowledge/methods/ie/micro-motion.md`): element-level improvement of a whole job is ECRS (FC-028); a time without the analysis is the time study (FC-019); a licensed motion-time system is PMTS (`ie.pmts` — left out); posture and force are the ergonomics tools. It is unsuitable for long, varied work with judgement in it, and refuses to supply a time.

**The list of classes — a closed list of fourteen**, Gilbreth's seventeen with three folded (checked against Barnes 1980 and Niebel & Freivalds 2014 ch. 4, *from knowledge — the books are not on file; see the record's `checked` line*): *Transport empty* is **reach** (TE); *Grasp* (G); *Transport loaded* is **move** (TL); *Position* (P), which takes *pre-position* (PP) in with it; *Release load* is **release** (RL); *Use* (U), which takes *assemble* (A) and *disassemble* (DA) in with it — the hands doing the work on the thing; *Inspect* (I); *Search* (SH); *Select* (ST); *Plan* (Pn); *Hold* (H); *Avoidable delay* (AD); *Unavoidable delay* (UD); *Rest to overcome fatigue* (R). The codes are Gilbreth's as the texts give them. Folding keeps the list small enough to pick on a phone; nothing is lost that the questions need (assemble, use and disassemble are all the hand at work).

**Established · assumed · chosen.** *Established:* the therbligs and their names and codes (Gilbreth & Gilbreth 1917; Barnes 1980; Niebel & Freivalds 2014); the principle that a hand used as a clamp, a search and a select are motions to question (Barnes's principles of motion economy: "do not use the hands for holding where a fixture can do it"; materials and tools in fixed places so there is nothing to search or select); that unavoidable delay and rest are allowed, not waste. *Assumed:* the seconds are what was timed or said (a stopwatch, the video study); the class and the hand are the observer's call; two elements marked *together* really did happen at the same time. *Chosen — Faultline's own, said on the page and in the working:* **the four groups** — **working motion** = reach, grasp, move, position, release, use (the motions that bring the work about, in the sense of the books' "accomplishes work" group; it is *not* the customer's value, which the value stream map judges); **checking** = inspect (counted apart: it is not waste and not working); **motion to question** = search, select, plan, hold, avoidable delay (the books' objective-hindering and non-working motions that a better layout, fixture or sequence can remove); **allowed** = unavoidable delay and rest. Other books group position and inspect differently; the choice is named so a reader can disagree. **A moment** is one element, or two elements marked together (one on each hand): its seconds are the **longer** of the two that have seconds (the hands work at the same time); the cycle is the sum of its moments' seconds. **A moment is working** if any of its timed elements is working; **to question** if every timed element is; else **checking or allowed**. The shares are of the cycle, so the three add to the cycle. A hold done while the other hand works costs no cycle time and is therefore *not priced*, only counted (below). **Money** (`knowledge/MONEY.md` §4, FC-233 as FC-028, labour L): the cycle's seconds that are only motion to question × cycles a shift ÷ 3,600 × what an hour costs (the line's wage with on-costs, offered) — labour on the non-working part, never a packs-lost figure (that needs the bottleneck; FC-028/FC-020 say it for the element removed). Each missing input is named and nothing waits on it.

## 4. Inputs
*Who or what* — text, optional, a label; the sentence says "Truck 3:". For each of up to 24 elements, appearing once the one above is classed: **what** (text, optional; blank it is "Element n"); **class** (choice of the fourteen, required for the first two — a cycle needs at least two elements — each with its code and meaning); **hand** (choice: Left hand · Right hand · Both hands · Whole body or truck; optional — blank it is not said, counted in the totals and in neither hand); **takes** (seconds, number ≥ 0, optional — an element with none is counted, named in a warning and left out of the time); **together with the one above** (choice, optional, shown for a left or right hand from the second element: *Yes — at the same time, the other hand*; blank it is *after the one above*). Then, all optional and grouped as money: **cycles a shift**, **what an hour of the person costs** (offered from the line's wage, `labourRate`), **shifts a week**. Nothing is read from the line but the wage; no default is a typical time.

## 5. Data suitability
The motions are timed on the floor or from the video study at the speed the work is done; one cycle may not be typical — the page says to time several and average (FC-019's cycles). A time is meaningful only for the element it names; the micro-motion level is below what a stopwatch resolves reliably (under about a second), so the caveat says the seconds of the short elements are rough and the video study at frame level is the better source. A pair marked *together* whose hands are not one left and one right is not a pair: the element is taken as after the one above and the page says so. Elements with no seconds are counted, never filled; a moment with one timed element and one untimed takes the timed one's seconds as a lower bound, said. With no seconds at all there is no cycle and no share, and the page says so and what to do (time it).

## 6. Calculations
Elements e₁…eₙ, each (class cᵢ, hand hᵢ, seconds sᵢ or none, together tᵢ). **Moments:** eᵢ joins the moment of eᵢ₋₁ when tᵢ, hᵢ and hᵢ₋₁ are one left and one right, and eᵢ₋₁ is not already the second of a moment; else it starts its own. A moment's seconds Mⱼ = max of its elements' sᵢ that exist (none: untimed, left out). **Cycle** C = Σ Mⱼ. **Class of a moment:** working if any timed element's group is working; to question if all timed elements' groups are; else other. **Shares:** W = Σ Mⱼ (working moments) ÷ C, Q = Σ (to-question moments) ÷ C, O = 1 − W − Q. **Longest element:** the greatest sᵢ (the first met on a tie). **By class and group:** counts of all elements and Σ sᵢ of the timed. **For each hand** (left, right; a *both* element counts for both): own seconds = Σ sᵢ; **holds** = Σ sᵢ of hold elements; **holds while the other works** = Σ sᵢ of a hold in a two-element moment whose other element is working; **nothing listed** = Σ Mⱼ of timed one-element moments whose only element belongs to the other single hand. **Money:** Q seconds × cycles ÷ 3,600 × rate; × shifts a week.

**Independent worked cases** (`scripts/knowledge/cases-kaizen.py`, section M, plain Python, worked from the rules above, never from the module). **M1 — an FLT driver, 10 elements, whole body:** reach 14 s, search 9, inspect 4, position 6, grasp 5, move 22, unavoidable delay 12, position 8, release 7, avoidable delay 6 → cycle **93 s**: working **62 s (66.7%)**, to question **15 s (16.1%)**, checking or allowed **16 s (17.2%)**; the longest is the 22 s move; at 40 boxes a shift and £15 an hour: **10 min and £2.50 a shift**, **£12.50** over 5 shifts. **M2 — a bench packer, 13 elements in 8 moments:** (right reach 0.8 / left reach 0.6), (right grasp 0.5 / left grasp 0.5), (right move 1.2 / left hold 1.2), (right position 0.7 / left hold 0.7), (right release 0.3 / left hold 0.3), (both move 1.0), (right inspect 0.9), (right avoidable delay 0.8) → cycle **6.2 s**: working **4.5 s (72.6%)**, to question **0.8 s (12.9%)**, other **0.9 s (14.5%)**; the left hand holds **2.2 s while the right works** and has its own 4.3 s, the right 6.2 s; the left hand has **nothing listed for 1.7 s**; at 4,000 cycles a shift and £14.40 an hour: **53.3 min, £12.80 a shift**, **£64.00** over 5 shifts. The same cases with no seconds: elements counted by class, no cycle, no share, no money.

## 7. Outputs
The **verdict** (the cycle's seconds, else the elements); the **sentence**; the **tiles** — *Cycle*, *Working motion*, *To question*, *Longest element*, *Elements*, *Labour a shift*; under the sentence the two hands' lines, the pairs ignored, the elements untimed; the **working** (a line per class present with its count and seconds, the three shares as sums, the hands, the money's sum); the **caveats**; **money** or what is missing; the **closing line**; and **the two-handed chart**: one row per moment down the page with its number and seconds, the left hand's, the right hand's and the both-hands / whole-body element in their columns (one column only when no element is of a hand), each element a box with its therblig's code and words — drawn solid with a heavy line when working motion, dashed when motion to question, plain when checking or allowed (never a state colour; every figure also in words).

**The report.** The shape's calculation paper from the same result: the band and verdict, the tiles, *What it says* (with the hand lines), the figures put in, **the two-handed chart** drawn from the same description as the screen (the rows, the codes, the seconds, the three line styles named in its caption), the working, the caveats, the money or what is missing, the closing line, and the notes and pictures-and-videos tail every calculation has.

## 8. Manufacturing usability
Phone first: the next element appears once the one above is classed; the choices are the big cards or chips of the shape; every control 44 px; nothing off the side; the chart inside its box. Said in the floor's words — *reach*, *grasp*, *move*, *line up (position)*, *set down (release)*, *hold*, *search*, *wait*. Gloves and noise: the whole cycle can be said to Line Talk and corrected on the page.

## 9. Failure handling
No class on the first element → the shape's *needed* refusal; **fewer than two elements** → the second element's class is asked for ("Choose “Element 2 is a”"); a negative or non-number seconds is refused by the box ("must be at least 0"). A pair that is not one left and one right is taken as sequential, said. An element with no seconds is counted and said; a moment with one untimed element is a lower bound, said. No seconds at all: counts only, no cycle or share, said. Money: each missing input named (*Give how many cycles a shift…*, *Give the line a wage…*), never invented, never blocking.

## 11. Verification

2. Case M2's moments (13 elements in 8), cycle (6.2 s), shares (72.6% · 12.9% · 14.5%), the left hand's 2.2 s held while the right works, its 1.7 s with nothing listed, and the money (53.3 min, £12.80, £64.00) equal the hand-worked figures — `micro.test.ts`, `micro-movements.mjs`.
3. With no seconds the elements are counted, no cycle, share or money is given and the page says so; one untimed element among timed ones is named and left out — `micro.test.ts`, `micro-movements.mjs`.
4. Both cases typed through the real boxes give the page's sentence, tiles, hand lines and money; the chart has a row per moment with each element's code, words and seconds in its column and the three styles; the paper read back carries the sentence, every element and the chart's caption — `micro-movements.mjs`, `calc-shape.mjs`.

7. Nothing in the tool supplies a time, a table or an AI answer; the page and the paper say so — `micro-movements.mjs` (the caveat read on the page and the paper) and `micro.test.ts` (no element is given seconds the input did not have).
