# FC-082 — Operational readiness: the specification

**Catalogue:** FC-082 (its neighbours: FC-080 handover and sign-off, available; FC-222 the skills matrix, built beside it; FC-168 the HACCP plan, not built) · **Knowledge:** `commissioning.operational-readiness` (full, practice) · **Asked for:** the catalogue's problem, "the line is handed over before people are trained and HACCP updated"; the stage gate's *Set up* and *Hand over* sections list it (`docs/METHODS.md` §7, §11; Rowland, 1 November: "I want all tools made that get used in the projects") · **Status:** built on `m6-stagegate` as a scored register's checklist; **written before the code.**

## 1. Purpose
Say whether production is ready to take a new line, item by item, and whose each open item is — the sentence the project lead can say to the plant manager that they could not say before: *"Readiness: 56 of 72 items ready; 16 open, 3 past their day. HACCP validation and cleaning validation would stop day one."* It is the half of commissioning most often left to the week after handover; the gate is every item, never an average.

**Scope.** A list of what must be ready, in areas, for one line or machine: each item with its **state** (Not yet · Ready · Not needed here), **who owns it**, **its day**, **what will prove it**, **what was seen** when it was done, and whether it **would stop day one**. It starts from a list for a **new line** (22 items in six areas — people, standards and documents, food safety and quality, maintenance and spares, planning and systems, health and safety), a starting point and never the standard; items are added, taken off with Undo and renamed. It says ready of total overall and by area, what is open by owner, what is past its day, which open items would stop day one, which open items are wishes (no owner or no day) and which ready items have no evidence written — as counts and names, never only a percentage. **Not here, and said on the page:** validating anything (the HACCP amendment and cleaning validation have their own evidence); the HACCP plan itself (FC-168, not built); who is signed off on which station (the skills matrix, FC-222 — a tick here, not joined); turning an open item into an action on the project; the count on the project's front page and the commissioning report (their parity with live is kept); a shorter list for a rebuilt machine (mark items *Not needed here*).

## 2. Users and scenarios
The project lead or site engineer, at a desk or in a handover meeting, starting the list at the project's start (not the last fortnight), giving each item an owner and a day, and reading it before the handover meeting. (1) *The record's worked case:* 72 items in six areas — people 14 of 18, SOPs 9 of 11, HACCP 3 of 4, spares 22 of 30, PM 6 of 6, cleaning 2 of 3: 56 ready, 16 open, 3 late, the HACCP validation and the cleaning validation the two that stop day one. (2) *A new line:* the starter list, nothing ready, red because food-safety items are marked as stopping day one. (3) *A rebuilt machine:* most of the list marked *Not needed here*. (4) *Everything ready:* green — "every item is done or not needed, so the gate is met" — and any ready item with no evidence written is named.

## 3. Method selection
An operational readiness review (ORR): the list of everything not the machine, each item with an owner, a day and its evidence, walked with the owners before the handover meeting (`knowledge/methods/commissioning/operational-readiness.md`; capital-project practice as process industries and FMCG run it — from knowledge, no single published standard). Alternatives from the record: the machine's own tests (acceptance tests, FC-074), the skills matrix (FC-222), the HACCP plan (FC-168), the spares and PM schedule (the maintenance tools). A score or percentage is refused as the figure: the record's own warning is that a percentage hides the one item that stops production.

**Established · assumed · chosen.**
- *Established:* items ready ÷ items, per area and overall, shown as a count; open items by owner and day, past due in red; the items that would stop day one marked as such (a food-safety item is never "nice to have"); the gate is every item, not the average; items without owners and days are wishes; "done" with no evidence is attendance, not readiness (the record). Independent case: `scripts/knowledge/cases-operations.py`, the readiness block (a second implementation, `python3 -I`): 72 items, 56 ready, 16 open, overall 78% — and the record says the gate is not 78%.
- *Assumed:* the team's word that an item is ready, and that a ready item's evidence exists where it says; "past its day" is a day before today; an item with no day is not late (it is a wish, counted).
- *Chosen (the builder, 1 November — recorded for the owner to overrule):* **the starter list** (22 items, wording and areas mine, from the record's own list — people trained, SOPs, HACCP, spares, PM schedule, cleaning, control plan, labels and codes, planning system, hygiene, health and safety, supply — never presented as the standard); **which starter items are marked "would stop day one"**: the food-safety and legal ones (HACCP amended and validated, cleaning validated, allergen controls, foreign-body controls, the risk assessment and PUWER sign-off), the operators and the cleaning team signed off, the SOPs and the labels and codes — every mark is the team's to change; **the verdict** is red when an open item would stop day one or any is past its day, amber for open items otherwise, green only when none is open; **a ready item with no evidence written** is said, never blocked; **the areas** are as typed (first seen, order kept; no area is "Other").
- *Money (`knowledge/MONEY.md` row FC-082 is "—": no money of its own):* the record's reading says an open item that stops day one costs the line's contribution for the time it stops it (row T). So, only while an item that would stop day one is open, and only as one output beside the counts, never the verdict: *"each day production cannot start is a day of the line not making: £X"*, from what the person types or the line's own day (`lib/lineLoss lineDay`, offered by one tap); missing — *"Give what a day of the line not making costs to say what a day's wait for start-up is worth"* — named, never blocking; no causation is invented beyond the record's.

## 4. Inputs
*What a day of the line not making costs, £* (optional; typed or the line's); per item: **the item** (text), **its area** (text; the areas already used are suggested), **state** (Not yet · Ready · Not needed here), **what will prove it** (text), **who owns it** (text), **by when** (a date), **what was seen** (text — the record's number), **would stop day one** (tick). Nothing is read from a project: the study is on a line and may be on a project.

## 5. Data suitability
An item with no words is not counted; a state that is not one of the three, or a day that is not a date, is refused in words and nothing is worked out on it; no items is refused ("Add at least one item"). An item *Not needed here* is neither ready nor open. A list of one item is a list. The page says plainly when a ready item has no evidence, when an open item has no owner or day, and when nothing is yet ready.

## 6. Calculations
total = items with words; ready, na, open by state; late = open and day < today; stoppers = open and marked; wishes = open with no owner / no day / either; noEvidence = ready with nothing in *what was seen*; areas in first-seen order with total, ready, na, open; owners = open items grouped by owner (case-insensitive; no owner is "Nobody yet", last among those first seen). Independent case P1 = the python case (72, 56, 16, 3 late, two stoppers, the six areas' counts); each is a unit test in `readiness/__tests__/readiness.test.ts`.

## 7. Outputs
Verdict *"16 open, 3 late"* (or *"Ready to hand over"*); the sentence (*"Readiness: 56 of 72 items ready; 16 open, 3 past their day. HACCP validation and cleaning validation would stop day one."*); tiles *Ready · Open · Past their day · Would stop day one · Without an owner or a day*; the lists **Would stop day one** (with owner and day), **Open, by who owns them**, **By area**; the money line while a stopper is open; what it does not say; the closing line (*"Not ready to hand over: 16 items open — chase … first, they stop day one, then the 3 past their day. An item with no owner and no day is a wish — give each both."*).

**The report.** The study paper (`lib/studiesPdf`, the scored paper): the band with the verdict and the tiles; the sentence; **What must be ready before production takes the line** — a table by area (area, item with *would stop day one*, owner, day, state with *late*, the evidence, and each area's count under it); the money; the three lists; what it does not say; the closing line. It reads the same module as the screen; nothing missing is made up.

## 8. Manufacturing usability
Desk or meeting more than phone: items as cards under their area, each a name, a state, who, when, what proves it and what was seen; on a phone the cards stack, controls are 44 px and nothing runs off the side; a removal is undone in one tap; a client reads and changes nothing.

## 9. Failure handling
Every refusal in words under the figures, the result absent until the list works; a missing cost named and the answer standing; a record from another build opens as it was.

## 10. Integration
Record: a study of tool `scored`, key `readiness` (`agreed.scored`: `rows` the items with `group`, `status`, `owner`, `due`, `evidence`, `note`, `stopper`; `dayCost`) — no new noun (BLUEPRINT §2 unchanged), no migration (`studies.agreed` is jsonb). Shown wherever a scored register shows: the Scored registers hub, a line's Tools and a project's Tools, the stage gate's *Set up* and *Hand over* sections (`src/project/method/methodTools`; it opens at `/scored/readiness`), the list on paper. **Not changed:** the project's hand-over list, the front page and the commissioning report; the open items are not made actions — the *Hand over* gate's own items stay the sign-off, and this list is what production needs beside it.

## 11. Verification
`readiness/__tests__/readiness.test.ts` (the python case; the counts, owners, lateness boundaries, evidence; the sentence, tiles, lists, money and table; every refusal; the starter list); `scripts/acceptance/readiness.mjs` (a list started through the sheet from the starter and the record's worked case through the page: the sentence, tiles and lists equal the module's and the python literals; an item given an owner, a day, its evidence and marked ready, one marked not needed, one added and one taken off with Undo; the money named, typed and the line's offered; kept after a reload; the paper downloaded and read back; the phone; a client changing nothing; the stage gate's Set up and Hand over sections opening it); `scored-shape.mjs` (the shape unchanged for the other registers).

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. Ready of total overall and by area, open, late, the stoppers and the owners equal the independent case — `readiness.test.ts` ("the python case"); `readiness.mjs` step *a* (the worked case through the page's own module and record).
2. The verdict, sentence, tiles, lists, closing line and money are the module's on the page and the paper — `readiness.test.ts` ("what it says"); `readiness.mjs` steps *b*, *g*.
3. A new list starts from the starter (22 items, six areas, every item open, food-safety items marked as stopping day one) and is red until they are done — `readiness.test.ts` ("the starter list"); step *b*.
4. An item given an owner and a day and marked ready moves the counts and the sentence; *Not needed here* is neither ready nor open; a ready item with no evidence is said — `readiness.test.ts` ("the counts"); step *c*.
5. Past its day only after the day; an item with no day is a wish — `readiness.test.ts`; step *c*.
6. Refusals in words — `readiness.test.ts` ("refused, in words"); step *d*.
7. The money: only while a stopper is open; named when missing; typed; the line's offered in one tap — `readiness.test.ts`; step *e*.
8. Kept after a reload; a removal undone; a client changes nothing — steps *f*, *h*.
9. The paper downloaded and read back equals the screen; the phone has nothing off the side and 44 px controls — steps *g*, *i*.
10. The stage gate's *Set up* and *Hand over* sections list it as built and open it — `methodTools.test.ts`; step *j*.
Not proved / not built: the HACCP plan; the link to the skills matrix; the count on the project's front page and the commissioning report; an open item as an action.
