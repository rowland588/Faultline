# FC-240 — Documents: the shape the methods' pages share — the specification

**Catalogue:** FC-240 (the shape and its hub) · carries FC-142 project charter, FC-139 SIPOC, FC-140 customer needs to CTQs, FC-141 Kano analysis, FC-150 8D report (each its own spec) · **Knowledge:** `knowledge/methods/sixsigma/charter.md`, `sipoc.md`, `voc-ctq.md`, `knowledge/methods/rca/eight-d.md`; `docs/METHODS.md` §11 ("a document — a charter, a SIPOC, a lesson, a report — is a definition in a shape the app already has, not a new page") · **Asked for:** Rowland, 1 November — *"I want all tools made that get used in the projects"*, *"all tools must have user guidance"* · **Status:** built on `m6-doc` (1 November; the integrator merges). **Written before the code.**

## 1. Purpose
The pages a method needs — a charter, a scope, what the customer needs, a corrective-action report — live in Word and email: nobody can see what is missing, who agreed it, or what it is worth, and each would be a new screen. The shape makes a page a **saved record on a line, a project or nothing**, with named boxes each saying what to put in and where on the floor to find it, the boxes still needed before it can be agreed, a reading worked out from what was typed (the one sentence, tiles, tables, the working, the money), its agreement by name and day, and a paper. The sentence it lets one side say to the other: *"Give-away on Line 3 from 4.1 g to 2 g by 31 March, worth £89,107 a year — agreed by the sponsor on 2 November."*

**Scope.** This build covers the **shape**: the record (a `ToolStudy` of tool `doc`: `agreed.doc = { def, signed? }`, the boxes in `facts.docValues`, the photos in `facts.docPhotos`, nothing derived stored); the **definition** every document is (`src/tools/doc/defs/<key>.ts`, found by `import.meta.glob`: its sections and boxes in the floor's words, what is needed, its checks, its reading, its worked examples from an independent source); the page (the hub `#/doc`, one kind `#/doc/:key`, one record `#/doc/:key/:id`); the paper (`lib/report/docPaper`, one generic page from the definition's own reading, photos whole); the wiring (the one hub entry in the Tools lists, a line's Tools page one row *Documents*, the line report, a method's section opening the kind itself); and the table-driven proof (`src/tools/doc/__tests__/doc.test.ts`, `scripts/acceptance/doc-shape.mjs`). It proves the shape with **five documents**, each specified on its own. Not in this build: a revision log (a signed document is locked and the owner reopens it — no history of what changed); two people typing different boxes of one document at once (the newer save of the boxes wins); photos in the line report's paper; the other documents the methods name (FC-149 is/is not, FC-225 SOP with photos, FC-224 one-point lesson, FC-152 fault tree, FC-223 TWI sheet — each a definition added later).

## 2. Users and scenarios
The continuous-improvement lead at a desk, a line leader on a phone beside the line, a sponsor reading before agreeing. (1) A give-away project starts: the **charter** — problem as a number, goal with a date, scope, team, worth — filled in, agreed by the sponsor, locked. (2) The scope is argued: the **SIPOC** on one page. (3) A retailer's wishes: the **CTQ table** with limits and where each comes from, then a **Kano** survey of twelve shoppers. (4) A complaint arrives: the **8D report**, D0 to D8, read from the project's own register, fishbone and actions. A client reads any of them and takes the paper; a project's documents list under its Tools.

## 3. Method selection
Why one shape: every document is *boxes → a reading with a threshold and a caveat → an agreement → a paper*, and each differs only in its boxes, its reading and its words — `knowledge/BUILD-SHAPES.md`. Alternatives: (a) one screen a document (rejected: `docs/STRUCTURE.md` §14, the catalogue is the one list of tools, and each page would be wired five times); (b) an attached file as the section's output (what `docs/METHODS.md` §11 allows when nothing is computed — these are computed, read by other parts or required by the method); (c) Line Talk writing the document (rejected: the only AI is the voice and the camera, `docs/DIRECTION.md`; a document's figures are formulas'). The shape takes only what is the same; a **definition** brings everything that is the method's own.

**Established · assumed · chosen.**
- *Established:* each document's content, formulas and thresholds are its own spec's with their sources. The shape itself holds no figure from a source.
- *Assumed:* what is typed is a person's; a reading is worked out again from the boxes and the definition *as it is now*, so an improved definition changes what an old record says, never what was typed.
- *Chosen (the builder, 1 November — told to choose and report):* (1) **the boxes are in `facts.docValues`, not `agreed`** — on a project the database keeps `agreed` for anyone but the owner once written (`supabase/STUDIES.sql faultline_keep_study_agreement`), so a team member's edit to a charter's words stored in `agreed` would be silently reverted by the cloud; `agreed.doc` holds only the kind and the owner's agreement, which *is* the owner's. (2) **Photos are `facts.docPhotos`, an id-keyed list** (merged across devices as `turns` and `readings` are), not an object under `facts.doc`, which two phones would overwrite whole; the files are named to the cloud by `studies.mediaKeys` and need `supabase/DOC_PHOTOS.sql` (a migration waiting — until it is applied the cloud refuses the files and they stay on the device that took them). (3) A document is **never refused for what is typed**: a box that is not a number, a limit the wrong way round, a list too long is said beside the box and under *To put right*, the document is saved as typed and is simply not ready to agree. (4) *Ready to agree* is every needed box filled — each definition says which are needed; the agreement is a name and a day, taken only by `can.agree` (the owner), and **locks** the boxes until the owner reopens it. (5) The status wears the state colours with its word: Draft grey, Ready amber (waiting on somebody), Agreed or Closed green — never red. (6) Money is each definition's own causation or the sentence saying there is none (`knowledge/MONEY.md`); every money input optional, a missing one named, nothing blocked. (7) A list has `alt` for the rare need met either of two ways (a Kano survey typed answer by answer *or* tallied by hand).

## 4. Inputs
Per definition (`DocField`): an id, a label in the floor's words, a kind (text, long text, choice, person, date, number), one line of help saying what to put in and where on the floor, an example, `required`, number rules (min, max, whole, unit), `showIf`, and `offer` (the project's lead, the line's wage with on-costs, a pack's materials — one tap, never filled in unasked). A **list** has columns (the same boxes) and a row word; **photos** are taken or chosen, kept the moment they are picked, each with a caption. The record is `facts.docValues` (a number kept as typed, read as a number), `facts.docPhotos` and `agreed.doc`.

## 5. Data suitability
Each box is checked on its own by the shape (a number that is not one, outside its range, not whole; a date that is not a date; a choice that is not an option; a list longer than it may be) and across boxes by the definition's `check` — advisory, listed under *To put right*. A box blank and needed is named under the status. A document whose definition is not in this build (a record from a newer phone) keeps its boxes untouched and says so, never throws. A client sees every box disabled and is told so.

## 6. Calculations
The shape does no sum of its own beyond the count of needed boxes filled (*Filled in n of m*) and the status. Each definition's formulas and independent cases are its own spec's and `scripts/knowledge/cases-doc.py`; `src/tools/doc/__tests__/doc.test.ts` runs **every** definition's worked examples, a blank document, the agreement and its record; `figures.test.ts` holds each formula to its independent literals.

## 7. Outputs
On the screen: the band with the status; the agreement box (who and when, or what is still needed, or the one button); the boxes by section; beside them the *Filled in* tile and the definition's tiles, the sentence and what else it says, *To put right*, the tables worked out, how they were worked out, the money (or why there is none), what it does not say; where it sits, *Used for*, the action plan and the closing line. On the paper (`lib/report/docPaper`, the same run): the band with the status, the tiles, the sentence, who agreed it and when (or what is still to fill in), what else it says and the checks, each section with its boxes (a blank one printed as blank, so a draft prints as the form it is), the lists as tables, the photos whole with captions, the tables, the working, the money, what it does not say.

**The report.** One generic document paper, on the report engine and the lean page, from the definition's own run: purpose and context (the kind, the record's name, where it sits, the day), the boxes as typed with units, the reading (sentence, tiles, tables, working), the money or the sentence saying why none, the limits, who agreed it and when. A list's paper (the hub's, a kind's, a line's or project's) is each document a page; the line report prints each saved document under its kind's name. It reads the same module as the screen; nothing missing is made up.

## 8. Manufacturing usability
Start from the kind's page or the hub card (≤ 3 taps to a first box: New, a name, the boxes); the reading is worked out as each box is typed, saved half a second after the last key (never a write for each); the phone is one column with every box thumb-sized and nothing off the side; a list's row is a card of its own boxes; a row or a photo taken out can be put back.

## 9. Failure handling
A definition that throws on what it did not foresee shows *It could not be read from what is typed yet* and the page stays up. A document whose kind is not in this build keeps its boxes. Offline: as every study (IndexedDB, synced by the engine). A client: read-only, takes the paper.

## 10. Integration
**Record:** a `ToolStudy` with `tool: 'doc'` (already in `STUDY_TOOLS` and the cloud's `studies_tool_check`, `supabase/SHAPE_TOOLS.sql`); `agreed` and `facts` are jsonb the mapper already carries, so **no column**; the one database change is the media rule (`supabase/DOC_PHOTOS.sql`, waiting). **Where it shows (CLAUDE.md rule 5):** the rail's Tools, the front door, a line's and a project's Tools, New ▾ and Jump to list *Documents* from the catalogue's registry; a line's Tools page has one row counting this line's documents with the latest status; a project's document is its own and lists in its Tools (`?project=`); the line report lists each as a record ticked in by default, titled by its kind, with the section the document's own paper prints; a method's section opens each kind itself (`methodTools openAt`, *A mode of Documents*, New starts that kind in the project); Delete says what goes. **Access:** `useAccess(projectId)`: `can.edit` types, `can.agree` agrees, `can.remove` deletes. **Pages:** `docs/PAGES.md` and `scripts/inventory/pages.mjs` have the hub, one kind's list and a record; `scripts/smoke.mjs` opens them; the seed has a charter ready to agree, a SIPOC and an 8D report.

## 11. Verification
`src/tools/doc/__tests__/doc.test.ts` (every definition: sound structure, examples read exactly as the independent literals say, a blank document, the agreement, a record; the reading of typed boxes, editing, the photos' file keys); `figures.test.ts` (each formula against `cases-doc.py`); the catalogue, registry, methodTools and sync-schema tests; `scripts/acceptance/doc-shape.mjs`, table-driven over every definition.

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. A definition added as one file in `src/tools/doc/defs/` is listed on the hub, opens at `#/doc/<key>`, and is covered by the unit test and the acceptance script with no change to either — `doc.test.ts` (the registry loop) and `doc-shape.mjs` (step 0 and *a*, which read the registry).
2. A new document is a Draft that says how many needed boxes are filled and names each still needed; nothing is said before there is something to say — `doc-shape.mjs` step *a*.
3. Every definition's worked examples, typed through the real boxes (text, a choice by its button, a list by Add and its cells), give the sentence, tiles, tables, money, checks and needs of the independent literals and of the definition's own reading — `doc.test.ts` ("read exactly as the independent literals say"); `doc-shape.mjs` step *b*.
4. The boxes are kept after a reload, in `facts.docValues` and not in `agreed` — `doc-shape.mjs` step *c*.
5. The document is agreed by name and day (the owner), locks, is kept after a reload, and is reopened; the status moves Draft → Ready → Agreed — `doc.test.ts` ("agreed only by a signature"); `doc-shape.mjs` step *c*.
6. The paper, downloaded and read back, has the status line (and *Agreed by … on …* once signed), the sentence, the tiles, every section, every box given, every list row, the tables, the money or why none, what it does not say — `doc-shape.mjs` step *d*.
7. The phone (390 px): nothing off the side, every box and Add/Remove thumb-sized — `doc-shape.mjs` step *e*.
8. A list row taken out and Undo puts it back where it was; photos are added with a caption, taken out and brought back, kept after a reload, in the media store, named to the cloud by `studies.mediaKeys`, and printed whole with their captions — `doc-shape.mjs` step *f*; `doc.test.ts` ("the files a document names").
9. Rename in More ▾, Delete (the ask says what goes) and Undo — back whole with its boxes and photos — `doc-shape.mjs` step *g*.
10. A line's Tools page has one row *Documents* (not a row a kind) counting its documents; the line report lists each as a ticked record and prints it — `doc-shape.mjs` step *L*.
11. A project's New is the project's, and the project's lead is offered, never filled in — `doc-shape.mjs` step *P*.
12. A client reads a project's document read-only, cannot agree it, is told so, and takes its paper — `doc-shape.mjs` step *C*.
13. A method's section opens the kind itself — `methodTools.test.ts`; `scripts/acceptance/methods.mjs` step *4a*.
14. No column: `src/cloud/__tests__/sync-schema.test.ts` and `scripts/check-live-schema.mjs` unchanged; the media rule's migration is written, **not applied** (below).
Not yet proved: the two-device sync of a document and of its photos (`scripts/sync-two-devices.mjs` is the integrator's gate); the photos reaching the cloud, which waits for `supabase/DOC_PHOTOS.sql` to be applied.

## 12. Maintainability
**How to add a document** — create `src/tools/doc/defs/<key>.ts` (a `DocDef`; copy `sipoc.ts`), put any arithmetic in `src/tools/doc/figures/<name>.ts` with a unit test against independent cases (a second implementation in `scripts/knowledge/cases-doc.py` or a published table), write `knowledge/specs/FC-nnn-<name>.md` from the template before the code, write its guide in `src/catalogue/guides/docs.ts`, and set its catalogue entry `form: 'mode'`, `partOf: 'FC-240'`, `built: docBuilt('<key>', …)`. Nothing else changes: not the router, the registry, the hub, the paper, the test or the script. The shape is `src/tools/doc/` (types, doc, registry, context, the screen, form and view) and `src/lib/report/docPaper.ts`.
