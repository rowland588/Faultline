# FC-153 — Stratification: where does the problem live? (a question of the Analysis tool)

**Catalogue:** FC-153 Stratification (a mode of FC-105 Analysis; the Pareto of stops by shift, product and money and the heat map by machine and day — `src/lib/paretoFromLog.ts`, `src/lib/intelligence/heat.ts` — stay as they were). **Knowledge:** `rca.stratification`, with `stats.chi-square-association` and `stats.anova` for the tests. **Programme:** `docs/METHODS.md` §11 (PDCA Plan, DMAIC Analyse, 8D D2 and D4, A3). **Asked for:** Rowland, 1 November 2026 — *"I want all tools made that get used in the projects."* **Status:** built 1 November 2026 (branch `m6-analysis`).

## 1. Purpose
A total hides where a problem lives. The same rejects, or the same readings, split by shift, line, machine, product or supplier show whether the problem is everywhere (a system cause) or in one place (a local cause) — the first and cheapest step of any root-cause work, before the fishbone. The sentence it lets one side say to the other: *"Nights carry 62% of the rejects from half of what was made — 0.80% against 0.50% — and together it is Night on Product A: 0.96% against 0.52% for everything else."*

**Scope.** One question in the Analysis tool (`kind: 'strat'`), asked first: **Where does the problem live — which shift, line, machine or product?** Records tagged with one to four factors (up to twelve levels each) — **counted** (made and rejected) or **measured** (a reading) — are split by each factor in turn: the rate or mean of every place with its interval, its share of the rejects against its share of what was made, the test of the whole split, the places that stand out, then the **two strongest factors together** as a table of cells. The number of splits tried is counted and the level divided by it; a place is named only when enough was counted in it. Entry by chips and boxes or by pasting rows from a sheet. Not in this build: a third factor in the cross (the two strongest only); the stops, losses and weigher heads of the line read in without retyping (the data is tagged by hand, or pasted — FC-052's Pareto of stops keeps its own splits); a rate over time (stops per hour — a counted exposure other than packs); an interaction model for measured data (the factorial FC-111 does that on purpose); handing the place found to Is / is not (FC-149, not built).

## 2. Users and scenarios
The ops lead at a desk after a week of reject counts; the shift lead on a phone typing a record at the line. (1) Rejects by shift and product from the reject bin's tally sheet — Nights reject more, but only on Product A. (2) Rejects by line, small counts — three lines, one worse, but with 3, 2 and 9 rejected the tool must say it cannot tell. (3) Give-away by shift, or fill weight by weigher head — a measured split. (4) A paste from the factory's own spreadsheet: shift, product, packs made, packs rejected.

## 3. Method selection
`rca.stratification`: rate per place = count ÷ its denominator; a proportion split is a table tested by χ² (`stats.chi-square-association`) and, for two places, Fisher's exact when an expected count is under 5; a mean split is the one-way ANOVA with Tukey (`stats.anova`, as the groups question does it); the splits tried are counted and the level divided by them (Bonferroni) — the record's caution; a place found is a place to look, not a cause. Alternatives and when not: one sharp problem's is/is-not → FC-149; ranking causes by size → the Pareto; where on the product the defects sit → defect concentration; whether a difference between two things is real → Analysis *Did it change*.

**Established · assumed · chosen.**
*Established:* Ishikawa's stratification among the seven QC tools; Pearson's χ², Cramér's V, standardised residuals, Fisher's exact test, Clopper–Pearson intervals, one-way ANOVA with η², Kruskal–Wallis, Levene, Tukey's HSD (the arithmetic is `lib/ie/relate`, `groups`, `stats`, already proved against SciPy in `relate.test.ts` and `groups.test.ts`); Bonferroni's correction for the number of tests tried; Cochran's rule for the χ² (80% of cells expect 5 or more, none under 1 — `tableTest`). The independent cases of §6 are SciPy's, `scripts/knowledge/cases-m6-analysis.py`.
*Assumed:* each record counted once; made and rejected counted the same way in every place; the tags were written when the record was taken (a record without its tags cannot be stratified afterwards — said, and the record left out of that split).
*Chosen by Faultline (not the textbooks'):* a place is named as where the problem lives only with **at least 5 rejected in it** (the record's "a stratum of 3 rejects says little"; Cochran's 5) and a residual beyond **+2** (the table question's ±2), and only if its factor passes at the level ÷ the splits tried; a place with fewer than 5 rejected **and** fewer than 5 expected is "too few to judge"; a measured place needs 3 readings (as the groups question) and stands out when Tukey sets it apart from **every** other place; the two factors in the cross are the two with the greatest Cramér's V (or η²), each cell tested against **all the other cells** at the level ÷ (factors + cells tried); factors are said to *run together* when Cramér's V between them over what was made is **0.5** or more; at most four factors, twelve levels each, thirty-six cells in the cross. None of these has been put to Rowland one by one.
*Money (`knowledge/MONEY.md` FC-153):* the worst place's gap × what one reject costs. The gap is the rejects in the named place above what the rest's rate would have given; the cost of a reject is typed (optional); a missing cost is named in a sentence of its own and nothing waits on it. A measured split is not priced here (the capability study prices give-away); no causation is invented.

## 4. Inputs
*Agreed before measuring:* what is measured (a name); counted or measured; the factors to split by (chips for Shift, Line, Machine, Product, Supplier, or the floor's own, one to four, no repeats); confidence 90 · 95 · 99%; for measured, the unit; what one reject costs (£, optional). *Measured:* a record — a level of each factor (a chip, or a new one by name) and, counted, **Made** and **Rejected** (whole numbers, rejected no more than made), or, measured, the reading; or rows pasted, one line each: the levels in the order agreed then the numbers, separated by tabs or commas (a level may hold spaces). A struck record is kept and left out.

## 5. Data suitability
Counted: a factor needs two levels and something rejected (and not everything); the χ² is trusted only by Cochran's rule — otherwise it is called an approximation and the place is not named, with the merge-or-count-more advice; two places with an expected count under 5 use Fisher's exact. Measured: two places of 3 or more readings. Records without a tag for a factor, readings that cannot be used (rejected over made, a non-number), and a factor of more than twelve levels are each said, with the count. Two factors that run together are said to.

## 6. Calculations
Per factor and place: rate = rejected ÷ made, Clopper–Pearson interval, share of the rejects, share of what was made, expected rejects = made × (all rejected ÷ all made), standardised residual (O − E) ÷ √E from the 2-column table [rejected, not rejected]; the whole split by `tableTest` (χ², degrees of freedom, p, Cramér's V); excess = rejected − made × (the rest's rate). Measured: `compareGroups` (F, p, η², Levene, Kruskal–Wallis, Tukey's pairs); Kruskal–Wallis leads when a group of 20 or more is not bell-shaped or Levene's p < 0.05. Cross: each cell against all the others by `twoShares` (the z test, Fisher when an expected count is under 5), or by Welch's t for readings. Level per factor = (1 − confidence) ÷ factors; per cell = (1 − confidence) ÷ (factors + cells).
**Independent cases** (SciPy, `scripts/knowledge/cases-m6-analysis.py`, each a unit test in `src/tools/analysis/__tests__/analysisStrat.test.ts`):
- *Rejects by shift and product* (the record's case: Day A 120/24,000, Day B 80/16,000, Night A 230/24,000, Night B 90/16,000): by shift χ² 27.8735, 1 df, p 1.29512e-7, V 0.01867, residual ±3.721, Night 0.80% against Day 0.50%, 62% of the rejects from 50% of the packs; by product χ² 11.6462, p 0.000643334, V 0.01207; Night A 0.9583% (230/24,000) against the rest 0.5179%, z 7.1045, p 1.2e-12, excess 105.714; Day A 0.5000% against 0.7143%, "below the rest"; Night B not different (p 0.137); level per factor 0.025, per cell 0.05 ÷ 6.
- *Three lines* (12/1,500, 9/1,400, 31/1,600): χ² 13.4471, 2 df, p 0.00120225, V 0.05466, residuals −1.281, −1.785, +2.910; exact 95% intervals 0.4140–1.3933%, 0.2944–1.2168%, 1.3201–2.7389%. *Small counts* (3/900, 2/800, 9/700): χ² 8.4575, p 0.0145706, one cell in three expecting 5 or more (66.7%), smallest expected 4.083 — said not to be trusted and no place named.
- *Three shifts' give-away* (the FC-107 case): F 21.9291, p 7.20559e-6, η² 0.67622, Tukey p A–B 0.000021, A–C 0.924719, B–C 0.000049: B stands out.
- *Running together*: shift × line made 30,000/10,000/5,000/35,000: Cramér's V 0.62994.

## 7. Outputs
The lean page: the band (the verdict — *Lives in Night*, *Lives in one combination*, *Not in one place*, *The shifts differ*, *Too few*), five tiles (Records · Made or Readings · Splits tested · Stands out · Strongest), the sentence, the picture (a lane a place with its interval, the leading — else the strongest — factor), the tables (*Every split tried*; *By <factor>*: made, rejected, rate, interval, share of the rejects, share of what was made, against chance; *Together*, each cell its rate and counts), the checks (records left out, counts too small, splits tried with the divided level, factors that run together, places too few to judge), the readings, Agreed, the action plan, the closing line. A place that stands out is **amber, never red** — a find is a place to look, not a failure; colour is never the only carrier (the words *more — stands out* are in the table). **Never claimed:** that where it shows is why it happens; that a place not shown to differ is the same; that a factor split alone is not another's doing when they run together.

**The report.** The paper (`src/lib/studiesPdf.ts analysisBlocks`, the report engine): the band and tiles, the sentence and what follows, the picture, every table above (the grids' widths are shares, so the columns sit in the page — a defect in the gauge R&R and attribute-agreement grids, whose widths were whole numbers, is corrected in the same line), the checks, what was agreed in words (*counted, split by Shift, Product*), and every record worded as the screen words it. It reads the same module as the screen.

## 8. Manufacturing usability
Phone first: a chip row a factor, a number box each for made and rejected, one primary button that says which level is still to pick; or paste the sheet. Taps from the question to an answer: pick the question, tap the factors, agree, paste or tap records, read. Controls a thumb's size.

## 9. Failure handling
No name, no factor, two factors of one name, a negative cost: refused in words while agreeing. No record: says what to add. One level, nothing rejected, everything rejected, too many levels, counts too small, fewer than 3 readings in a place: each said in the checks and the sentence, never a figure invented. A client reads and changes nothing (`can.edit`, `can.agree` as the Analysis tool's).

## 10. Integration
Record: `ToolStudy` of tool `analysis`, `agreed.analysis` (`kind: 'strat'`, `strat: { factors, data, costEach? }`), readings in `facts.readings` with a new optional `tags` per reading — inside the existing JSON, no migration. Shows wherever the Analysis shows: its list (a row: *where it lives*, the verdict, *n records*), a line's and a project's Tools page, the method pages (PDCA Plan, DMAIC Analyse, 8D D2 and D4, A3 Analysis — FC-153 opens the Analysis marked as a mode), the directory entry (`#/tools`, FC-153, Open). Access as the Analysis tool's. No new page, route or menu line.
