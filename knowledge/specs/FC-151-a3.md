# FC-151 — A3: the problem and its solution on one page — the specification

**Catalogue:** FC-151 · the A3 method's own sheet and paper (`docs/METHODS.md` §16) · **Knowledge:** `knowledge/methods/rca/a3.md` (Sobek & Smalley 2008, *Understanding A3 Thinking*; Shook 2008, *Managing to Learn* — from knowledge, texts not re-read) · **Asked for:** Rowland, 1 November — *"I asked you to build the method and insert the tools where required"*; the 6M told on one page for the site meeting · **Status:** the board and paper built 1 November on `m7-a3`; **this specification written after them on `t8-1`** (the standard says before — said here, and the criteria below were then proved against what stands, `method-a3.mjs` and `a3Model.test.ts`).

## 1. Purpose
The story of an improvement is spread over many pages. The A3 is the whole of it on one sheet read top-left to bottom-right, in seven blocks, so the owner and the people who must support it can question each block: *"Lane 3 leakers 3.2% → 0.2% in four weeks; cause the new jaw's heater, verified; two countermeasures done; held."*

**Scope.** The A3 as the *A3 method's* page (`#/project/:id`, the board of two columns — how it is now: Background, Current condition, Target; what is to be done: Analysis, Countermeasures, Plan, Follow-up) and as one paper, *A3 on one page* (A3 landscape, Report ▾). The header (the problem in one line, the owner and who must agree), the seven blocks written as words on the method's sheet (project notes `method:a3:*`), the countermeasures as a list (what · who · by when · against which cause, rows `counter`, `counter@2`…), the project's own records speaking read-only: the newest study that shows the number at the current condition, a different later one at the follow-up, and the project's fishbone at the analysis. The closing line counting the blocks written. Not here: the drawn chart with the target as a line (the number is read from the tool's own sentence, one tap from its picture); a status or proposal A3 (the same shape with the blocks renamed — the record); the dialogue itself (the agreement is a line of words: who has questioned it and the day); any money of its own.

## 2. Users and scenarios
The line's owner of a chronic problem, with the sponsor, at the site meeting. (1) Leaking seals on lane 3 — the record's worked case. (2) A sheet half-written: what is missing is said. (3) A page too full for one A3 at a readable size: it runs on to a second page and says so, nothing cut.

## 3. Method selection
The record's procedure: the blocks in their places; every cause has a countermeasure or a reason not, and every countermeasure names its cause; the follow-up is the before → after on the number; the owner drafts and the sponsor and the people affected question each block. Alternatives (the record): the customer's formal report → the 8D (FC-150); the cause-finding itself → the fishbone and five whys (FC-146); the proof's arithmetic → PDCA. Unsuitable: a programme (a status A3 reads differently) — said in the guide.

**Established · assumed · chosen.**
- *Established (the record):* seven blocks and their order; the current condition and the follow-up read against the one number; countermeasures name the cause they answer, one that names none is a to-do list; a follow-up block left empty is an A3 that was never finished (the page lives until it is written); the A3 is for one problem.
- *Assumed:* the words written are facts (the sheet asks how each cause is known — measured, counted, observed or reported); the people named agreed.
- *Chosen:* (1) nothing is needed to *write* a block — the sheet is words, and the closing line counts: **n of 7 blocks written**, with the follow-up named when it is open and the countermeasures that name no cause counted. (2) A countermeasure is one answer, `what | who | by when | against which cause`; an older answer with no separator reads as the whole "what". (3) The newest study of Capability, the Control chart, the Loss count, the map or Work sampling speaks at the current condition; the newest of Analysis, the chart or Capability that is **not** that one speaks at the follow-up — one study is never both, a before-and-after needs two. (4) The fishbone's causes speak at the analysis: how many, which are marked root with their whys, and which root **no countermeasure names** (a root and a countermeasure's cause match when one text contains the other, ignoring case). The fishbone keeps the causes; the sheet keeps nothing of them. (5) A sheet longer than one A3 holds at a readable size runs on to a second page and says so on the paper — a Faultline choice (nothing is cut).
- *Money (MONEY.md §4, FC-151):* the A3 has no pound figure of its own. The Background asks for the gap's cost in money *where it is known* and says where it came from; the sheet does not make one up and does not nag when there is none. The problem's £ on the left and the countermeasures' worth on the right are the 6M's (FC-146) when the project holds a 6M; this tool does not add a second cost model.

## 4. Inputs
None needed to start. Written: *The problem, in one line*, *Owner — and who must agree it*, *Why it matters*, *The facts, drawn, with the number*, *The number now, with its unit*, *The number wanted, by when*, *The root cause, with the evidence*, *How each cause is known*, the countermeasure rows, *The steps*, *The number checked — did it hold?*, *When it is checked*, *What it showed*, *Who has questioned it and agreed — and the day*. Read: the project's studies and fishbone.

## 5. Data suitability
Said, never refused: nothing written; a countermeasure that names no cause; a follow-up not written; no study of the number; no before-and-after; a root the countermeasures do not name.

## 6. Calculations
written = blocks with any word (the countermeasure block counts when a row is written); noCause = written rows with no cause; bare roots = roots no row names. **Independent cases** (`scripts/knowledge/cases-doc.py` A1; `a3Model.test.ts`): the lane-3 page — six of seven blocks written, two countermeasures both naming the heater, none naming no cause, the follow-up open; a second root "film tension" with no countermeasure is named bare.

## 7. Outputs
The closing line (*6 of 7 blocks written · the follow-up is not written — the page lives until it is.*); each block's words; under the current condition and the follow-up the tool's own title and sentence with Open it; under the analysis the fishbone's sentence (*2 causes on the fishbone, 1 marked as the root: … Every root has a countermeasure that names it.*); the countermeasure table.

**The report.** *A3 on one page* from Report ▾: A3 landscape, the header, the seven blocks in two columns in Toyota's order, each block's words whole, the tools' own sentences at the current condition, analysis and follow-up, the countermeasure table, the closing line — from `a3Model`, the one module the board reads, so the page and the paper cannot part. One page when it fits at a readable size; otherwise it runs on and says so.

## 8. Manufacturing usability
Two columns at a desk, one on a phone; every control 44 px; a block's help is its question; the fishbone and the project's Actions one tap from their blocks.

## 9. Failure handling
A project with no studies or no fishbone says so in the block and nothing waits; a client reads and writes nothing; a failed read of the fishbone leaves the analysis block as it was.

## 10. Integration
The A3 method's board (`project/method/shapes/A3Board`), its paper (`lib/report/a3Paper`, from Report ▾ and the Reports sheet), the method sheet (`lib/methodSheets/a3`), the project's studies (Capability, Control chart, Loss count, Analysis…), the fishbone (`listProjectCases`), the Actions. It writes only the sheet's notes.

## 11. Verification
`src/project/method/shapes/__tests__/a3Model.test.ts` (the model, the worked case and the fishbone's sentence), `src/lib/__tests__/sixmReportPdf.test.ts`, `scripts/acceptance/method-a3.mjs` (the board, the answers kept after a reload, the countermeasure list, the tools falling in and the fishbone speaking at the analysis, the paper downloaded and read back, a client, the phone).

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. The board has the seven blocks in two columns in Toyota's order, with the header and the closing line counting the blocks written — `a3Model.test.ts`, `method-a3.mjs` steps 1–2.
2. Countermeasures are a list of what · who · by when · against which cause, kept after a reload, and one naming no cause is said — `a3Model.test.ts`, `method-a3.mjs` step 3.
3. The project's latest study speaks under the current condition in the tool's own words and opens; the follow-up says there is no before-and-after and offers one — `method-a3.mjs` step 4.
4. The project's fishbone speaks at the analysis, naming the roots and any root no countermeasure names, on the board and on the paper — `a3Model.test.ts`, `method-a3.mjs` step 4b.
5. *A3 on one page* is one A3-landscape page with every word of the board, and a sheet too full runs on and says so with nothing cut — `method-a3.mjs` step 5.
6. A client reads and writes nothing; the phone fits with 44 px controls — `method-a3.mjs` steps 6–7.
Not done, and said on the entry: the drawn chart with the target as a line (the number is read, the picture is one tap away); the countermeasures are not read from the project's Actions (they are written on the sheet and the Actions are one tap away); the problem's money is typed in the Background, not read.

## 12. Maintainability
Model `src/project/method/shapes/a3Model.ts` (+ `a3Causes.ts` for the fishbone read); board `A3Board.tsx`; paper `src/lib/report/a3Paper.ts`. When the project's Actions are offered as countermeasure rows, the *limits* shrink.
