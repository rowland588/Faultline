# The room script — presenting Faultline to a customer

One page. Read it twice before the room; then leave it in your bag.
The demo workspace is the deck — you never open slides.

**The easy mode:** on the demo board, press **▶ Watch the demo**. The
tutorial film shows the whole app used start to finish, automatically, with
captions explaining each function — play it to the room, then do beats 2
and 5 live yourself. The beats below are for driving it freehand; the
numbers and objection answers apply either way.

**The promise, word for word:** *"Your OEE tells you the number. Faultline
tells you what the number is made of — what happened, where, how often,
and what it cost. Numbers and eyes."*

## Pre-flight (five minutes, before anyone arrives)

1. Sign in → home page → **Build / rebuild the demo workspace**. Fresh data,
   every number below guaranteed on screen.
2. Open **Demo — Packing Hall → Analyse** and leave it there. That's your
   opening shot.
3. Phone charged, on the same account — you'll capture live in beat 2.
4. Wifi dies? Nothing changes: the app is offline-first. Say so out loud —
   it's a feature, not a save.

## The seven beats (~12 minutes)

**1 · The board** — you're already on it.
> "This is last week on one packing line. Every bar is a machine, in hours
> **and pounds** — not percentages."

Press the **Frequency** chip, then **Both**: "Same data, three questions."
Pause on Both: "Rare-but-costly and frequent-but-quick are different
diseases. One chart shows you which you've got."

**2 · Capture** — switch to your phone, Capture tab.
> "Here's where the numbers come from. Watch — three taps."

Tap a machine, tap a problem, **Log now**. Back on the big screen: the bar
just moved. *This is the wow. Let the room see it land.*

**3 · Walk into the worst bar** — tap the tallest bar, then its tallest bar.
> "No report requested. No analyst. You just walked from 'we lose money' to
> 'allergen changeovers on this line' in two taps."

Scroll to the prize line: **"This problem runs at £61k a year. Halve it and
you've banked £30k. That's the business case, priced by the floor."**

**4 · The open case** — back to the board, tap the 📌 *Allergen changeovers* pill.
> "One problem, one page — an A3 that fills itself. Five whys to the root
> cause. And this study is re-measuring the fix **live** — 6 of 10 samples in.
> Nothing gets called an improvement here until it's re-measured."

**5 · The receipt** — open the ✓ *Film snags* case (or the 🏆 pill).
> "6.9 minutes an event before. 2.6 after. Minus 62%, £5.1k a year — with
> both sample sizes on the page. This is a receipt, not a claim. Your FD
> will believe this number."

**6 · The meeting** — Meeting tab.
> "Monday morning, zero prep. The number, where it hurt, who's carrying
> what — every action aged and due-dated. Nobody wrote this. It was written
> by last week."

Arrow through the acts. Stop on Actions: point at an overdue one. "It ages
in red until someone closes it. That's accountability without a spreadsheet."

**7 · The eyes** — Snags → the Flow wrapper → play the walk footage.
> "The other half: film the line on a phone, freeze it, pin the fault to
> the machine itself. OEE could never see this. This is the 'eyes' half of
> the promise."

**Close:**
> "Give me one line and a fortnight. If the board doesn't pay for itself in
> found losses, we shake hands and part friends."

## Objections, answered in one breath

- **"We already have OEE."** — "Keep it. OEE is the thermometer; Faultline
  is the diagnosis. Your OEE says 62% — this says *why*, in pounds."
- **"Operators won't log."** — "Three taps, thumb-sized, ten seconds. And
  they see their bar move — the board gives back."
- **"We have an audit app."** — "Audit apps have eyes and no numbers. Where's
  the Pareto? Where's the £?"
- **"What's the setup?"** — "Type your machine names in. That's it. No
  integrations, no sensors, no IT project — it works offline on the phones
  you already own."
- **"Our wifi is terrible."** — "Perfect: it's offline-first. It syncs when
  it can; the floor never waits."

## If it goes wrong

- Demo data looks stale or mangled → home page → **rebuild the demo** (20 s).
- You forget a line, or the room wants the overview first → press
  **▶ Watch the demo**: the film walks every function, in order, narrated,
  the same every time. Pick up live from wherever it lands.
